## II. Evaluation — per joint

**E1 (J1) — The audit's dilemma is the right instrument, and its dichotomy is false: there is a third category.** The audit forces: either the aspect is a *real* distinction (then T7 breaks) or a *merely display-level* one (then Lemma 1 demotes and P1 loses its ultimate anchor). But the scholastic/Advaitic tradition — which the audit itself invokes as "not formalized" — contains the precise intermediate category: the **formal (virtual) distinction**, *distinctio rationis ratiocinatae* — a distinction in reason **with foundation in reality**: two formalities, one reality. Under it: aspects are *formalities founded in A*, not *res* — no second reality (T7 safe: relations require res-terms), no parts (T1 safe: parts are res), numerically identical *in re* (D6 safe: self-"relation" = identity because the relata were never two beings). The audit's horn (b) is thereby defused rather than absorbed: internal articulation-as-structure is NOT a relation in T7's sense **iff the relata are not res** — that is the sharp criterion requested. Steelman: S1. Its demotion option is not merely weak — it is **internally fatal** (a display-level Lemma 1 cannot support P1 [Ultimate]: A's self-knowledge would be contentless at the ultimate tier, against T9/D1 at that tier). Its promotion option (A5) is not wrong — it is the correct *contingency*, not the current move.

**E2 (J2) — Adopt wholesale.** Cumulative-case prominence is free and accurate; no doctrine change.

**E3 (J3) — Adopt; the proposed canonical sentence is basically the correct repair.** Sharpening: the word "M" is itself subject to the two-truths rule (D12) — M-as-P-present (complete, in A) and M-as-F-schema (open, for us). Wiring D10 to M's status directly is the completion the audit half-performs.

**E4 (J4) — The audit's diagnosis is right; its surgery targets the wrong organ — demotion would be cosmetic.** Reason: **T11 derives beauty from A1 + perfection-predication, not from A4.** Demote A4 and beauty re-enters via T11 unchanged. The true soft joint — which neither v5 nor the audit names — is the **perfection-predication license**: the suppressed rule "X is a perfection + A1 ⇒ A has X." The complete repair (S3): define the license restrictively — a *perfection* is an attribute whose **absence would constitute an A1/A3-violating lack**. Then beauty qualifies only insofar as beauty = maximal order/intelligibility (already D7's reading: "supports as maximal order"), i.e., A4's metaphysical content **overlaps A3** (declare this), and its independent remainder — the aesthetic-inflection "mystery is a defect" — is **[aesthetic-regulative]**, never an inference license ("beauty ⇒ truth" is not licensed; RP7). Verdict: **keep A4 as axiom with the three declarations** (license defined; A3-overlap owned; aesthetic use tagged). This is strictly stronger than the audit's fork and touches no derivation.

**E5 (J5) — Adopt; the worked example is convertible into a research instrument** (S4): the Born-rule example states precisely what would count as progress — an intra-M necessity theorem (Gleason-type: measure forced by Hilbert structure) demonstrates P-presence *instance-by-instance within the web's grammar*, while never constituting from-A derivation. This turns J5 from shield into method.

**E6 (J6) — Adopt verbatim:** grounding claim, not constants-explanation. One sentence, placed at J6.

**E7 (J7) — Adopt:** bridges = heuristic-value evidence; empirical value accrues only to bridges that survive hostile tests.

**E8 (Overall) — The audit's final profile is correct and worth adopting as the registry's own summary,** with two corrections: J4 is not "one redundant axiom" but "one under-declared license plus one declared redundancy"; and its J1 verdict ("promote or demote") becomes "sharpen with the formal distinction; pre-commit promotion (A5) as the hostile-review contingency; demotion refused as internally fatal."

## III. Steelman

**S1 — J1 completed with the formal distinction.** Criterion installed in v6 §5: articulation is a distinction **with foundation in reality and without addition of reality** — (i) non-partitive: aspects cannot exist apart and the fusion of A with its aspects is A, not A+1 (no addition of being); (ii) non-relational: the relata are formalities, not res, so no T7-violating pair of terms; (iii) sufficient: self-knowledge requires only internal contrast (formal distinction), not otherness — otherness is what the vantages add (D12). Comparative anchors [Comparative]: Aquinas's divine attributes (distinct *secundum rationem*, identical *in re*); Advaita *svaprakāśa* (self-luminosity without self-objectification — T9's own anchor); *sat-chit-ananda* as the paradigm formal triad. Status decision: **Lemma 1 kept as defended premise with the sharpened criterion; promotion to A5 pre-committed IF the criterion fails hostile review (W3/W6); demotion refused** (internally fatal, E1). The tradition resources the audit said were "not formalized" are now carried by the criterion itself.

**S2 — J3 completed with the two-tier reading of M.** Canonical sentence: "M is **complete qua P-present** in A's self-knowledge, and **indefinitely extensible qua F-schema** in any finite formalization; 'schema' marks our access, never a lack in A." Cross-wired to D10 and D12. Kills the V4 misreading at the root.

**S3 — J4 completed via the perfection-predication license** (E4's restricted definition + three declarations + no-inference-license clause). The audit's charge that the middle position "invites elegance⇒truth" is answered by an explicit **non-license**.

**S4 — J5's worked example (Born rule).** Stated in v6: P-present = the Born measure is contained in A's self-knowledge as part of this display's form; not F-deducible = no finite derivation from the axioms exists or is claimed; what finitude *can* produce is intra-M necessity (Gleason-type: the measure forced by Hilbert structure) — such a theorem shows necessity *within the web's grammar*, never from-A derivation; the remainder ("why this web") stays at J6.
