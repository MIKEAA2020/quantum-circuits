# PART I — THE STRICT FORMALISM

## 0. Status

This is an **axiom system**. Given the axioms, the propositions follow necessarily; the axioms are metaphysical commitments, not theorems of neutral logic. The system is internally coherent and generative. It is a rationalist terminus.

Three honest declarations (v2, kept):

1. **Ontological gap.** The axioms define and commit to the Absolute; they do not prove its existence from neutral premises. Accepted with supporting arguments — not demonstrated such that refusal is irrational.
2. **"Complete" means no doctrinal remainder** — not formally complete (Gödel; see P8).
3. **No suchness remainder.** "Suchness of maya" and "not deducible from the Absolute alone" are retracted (chat turn 136). No brute facts in the display; the only boundary is **epistemic**.

Every proposition in Part I is tagged **[Ultimate]** or **[Appearance]**. Nothing in Part II is axiomatic; Part II inherits E2's tagging discipline but its contents are all regulative **[Regulative]**, **[Appearance-illustrative]**, or **[Comparative]**.

## 1. Primitives

- **A** — the Absolute. **[Ultimate]**
- **D** — appearance, the display. **[Appearance]**
- **M** — the totality of mathematical truth, read as an **indefinitely extensible schema**, not a completed object. Quantification over M is schematic; no set-theoretic closure is assumed (J3).

**Derived (not primitives — D5 applied to the system itself):** **C** ≡ the self-luminosity of A (D1, T9). **P** ≡ the polarity template (D4).

## 2. Definitions

**D1 — Self-luminous.** A knows itself by identity: A = A. Its self-knowing is not a relation between two things; it is the identity of knowing and being. *(Defines C.)*

**D2 — Appearance.** D is the self-display of A; not a second reality; A appearing as multiplicity.

**D3 — Mathematical.** X is mathematical iff X has structure: identity, relation, distinction, order, symmetry, correspondence.

**D4 — Polarity.** A structure is polar iff it has one identity, two modes, a parameter, and an invariant. The modes are not separate realities; they are the identity under two aspects. *(Defines P.)*

**D5 — Parsimony.** The generative principle of D: no unnecessary additions, no arbitrary exclusions; the character of the mapping A → D; not an attribute of A, but intrinsic to A's self-expression.

**D6 — Virtuous circularity.** The form of A's self-identity: self-knowledge is identity, not a knower/known relation; any account of it is circular because there is no external vantage point; not a loop of dependence.

**D7 — No preference.** A has no preference among structures, laws, or possibilities. *Justification:* a preference would require a selective reason; one outside A violates A2; one inside A that excludes a consistent structure would be an arbitrary boundary — a defect of intelligibility (A3) and a partiality/lack (A1). Beauty (A4/T11) supports as maximal order but does not carry the load (J4).

**D8 — Plenitude.** All consistent structures appear in D; follows from no preference (no exclusion without preference).

**D9 — Simplicity → Parsimony → Complexity.** Simplicity is an attribute of A (T1) — what A *is*. Parsimony is the character of the intrinsic mapping rules (D5) — *how* simplicity expresses as structure. Complexity is the display — the articulated *result*. Complexity does not conflict with parsimony; it is produced by it.

**D10 — Two senses of "deducible."**
- **F-deducible** (finite): derivable by finite reasoning in a finite formal system. Nothing of this richness is F-deducible by finite perspectives (Gödel).
- **P-present** (present-in-knowledge): contained in A's complete self-knowledge — belonging to M as a necessary truth of the whole.
"Deducible in principle" always means P-present, never F-deducible (J5).

**D11 — Three senses of "real." (New in v3 — from the chat's process dialectic, S9.)**
- **R1, real-as-actual:** actual *within* the display (a stone falls; a manuscript is revised; experiences occur).
- **R2, real-as-ultimate:** belonging to the ground — to A itself.
- **R3, real-in-the-strongest-sense:** that which is, context-free. Only A.
*Usage rule:* unqualified "real" means R3. Most disputes about process, change, time, and perfection are R1-vs-R3 vocabulary collisions; naming the sense dissolves them.

**D12 — Perspective and two truths. (New in v3 — S26, S66.)**
- A **perspective** is a localized view within D. Localization is not a brute accident: completeness → self-knowledge → structure → web of correspondences → perspectives (a rational reconstruction of why perspectives exist — persuasive, flagged as reconstruction, not demonstration).
- A is **transjective**: beyond the subject/object split, which is display-internal. Conventional "objective" and "subjective" both live inside D.
- **Two-truths rule:** a statement may be true at display level and false or unformulable at ultimate level; the tier must always be named.

## 5. The articulation premise and the derivation of appearance

**Lemma 1 — Self-knowledge requires structure (defended).** Pure undifferentiated simplicity cannot be an object of knowledge; self-knowledge requires internal articulation. *Defense:* (i) articulation is **aspectual, not partitive** — a part can exist apart, an aspect cannot; T1 denies parts, not aspects; (ii) otherwise either A lacks self-knowledge (against T9) or "knowledge without anything structured to be known" is empty (against D1); (iii) articulation is not external relation (T7) — it is self-relation, i.e., identity (D6). *Honest residue:* that aspectual articulation does not compromise simplicity is the classical non-dual move; coherent, necessary, and the exact joint an adversary presses (J1).

- **P1 — Appearance is necessary. [Ultimate]** Self-luminosity (T9) + structure of self-knowledge (L1) + structure is mathematical (D3) ⇒ A's self-knowledge is M; M viewed from within is D. ∎
  - **Corollary 1.1 (Option A; S48).** This necessity is **non-temporal and non-productive**: appearance is not emitted, created, or sequentially emanated; it is the shining itself. "When did appearance begin?" is malformed — beginningless. No Neoplatonic emanation sequence sneaks in.
- **P2 — Appearance is non-dually identical with A. [Ultimate]** D is A's self-knowledge; not a second reality (A2); A appearing as multiplicity (D2). ∎
  - **Corollary 2.1 (asymmetric grounding; S5).** D grounds in A; A does not depend on D. The apparent circle ("no A without display, no display without A") is display-level mutual manifestation (rope-snake), not ultimate co-dependence. Grounding is one-directional and non-temporal.
- **P3 — Appearance is mathematical. [Ultimate]** D has structure (self-knowledge); structure is mathematical (D3). ∎
- **P4 — Appearance is polar. [Appearance]** Fundamental polarity: identity/appearance; every genuine structure exhibits the D4 template; D is structured (P3). ∎
- **P5 — Appearance is parsimonious. [Appearance]** No preference (D7) ⇒ no arbitrary additions; D unfolds per D5. ∎
  - **Corollary 5.1 (identity of unfolding; S52).** Unfolding is **identity under limitation, not production**: complexity = simplicity unfolded; time = timelessness unfolded; space = spacelessness unfolded — instances of A=A. Limitation is required for viewing; the whole is self-referential; **polarity is the signature of limitation.**

- **P8 — The specific form of D is P-present in M. [Ultimate]** M is the totality of A's self-knowledge; D is that totality laid out; every feature of D is present in M — ontologically necessary. No feature is F-deducible by finite perspectives (Gödel, D10). *The boundary is in us, not in reality.* ∎

# PART II — THE RESEARCH PROGRAM

*Everything in Part II is **[Regulative]**, **[Appearance-illustrative]**, or **[Comparative]**. None of it is axiomatic. None of it carries weight for Part I. It exists because the framework is meant to steer work — under the E3 lighthouse discipline: a lamp, not an engine.*

## RP0 — Mission statement

The Theory of Everything is the totality M itself — the web, not a finite Lagrangian (P8, P20). The framework cannot hand physics its node; it can tell physics what every genuine node **looks like** (D4) and where the corresponding structure lives (§7). Research under this framework is therefore: (i) mapping the web, (ii) instantiating the template in new domains, (iii) engineering falsifiable bridge claims from the template, and (iv) keeping the map honest (E1–E7).

**The revised generativity law (v5).** Generative power grows through specific, checkable commitments *and* through unification, formalization, and heuristic fertility. But empirical **predictive** power is gated by falsifiable surface — nothing bypasses the gate (**Popper clause**; this is all that survives of the v4-era one-liner "generativity grows exactly as fast as the capacity to be wrong…" — its "exactly as fast, and no faster" conflated generativity with predictive power and was corrected on evaluation). A protected hard core is tolerable only while a **novel-fact pipeline** stays open; a protected core with an empty pipeline is degenerating, whatever its scope (**Lakatos clause**; pipeline inspected at the W5 annual re-audit).

## RP1 — The generative methodology (operationalized P21)

**The template.** For any structure: one identity, two modes, a parameter of limitation, an invariant.

**Seven generator heuristics** (drawn from the chat's generative phase; each tagged):
1. **Noether lens** — behind every conservation law, find the symmetry; conserved = identity persisted through modes. *Established.*
2. **Maximal symmetry + breaking** — locate the largest symmetry a system permits; classify the breaking chain; asymmetry = display-level limitation. *Established method.*
3. **Duality hunting** — where two extreme regimes of a theory coincide structurally, predict a duality (S/T/U-duality, mirror symmetry, Langlands-type correspondences). *Established track record; each instance graded separately.*
4. **Point–circle template** — in each system, identify the r-parameter of limitation and the r = 0 ground; ask what "radius zero" means here. *Heuristic.*
5. **Algebraic completion** — nature prefers closed algebraic structure over fragments: complex numbers, spinors, octonion-adjacent structure appear wherever physics deepens. *Heuristic with strong track record, not a law.*
6. **Gauge principle** — interactions arise from requiring global symmetry to hold locally. *Established.*
7. **Supersymmetry-class unification** — fermion↔boson as a template instance. *Open — unconfirmed; strictly a candidate (E5).*
8. **H8 — the collapse diagnostic (new in v4, from the enhancement agenda, sugg. 5).** In any domain, locate where one pole of a genuine polarity has been mistaken for the whole; predict the missing pole and what its reintegration looks like (precedent: chronos-only circularity → wheel completion, S70). **Counter-criteria (mandatory):** (i) independent evidence of dysfunction (anomaly, inconsistency, measurable loss) — never bare template-scarcity; (ii) the missing pole specifiable in the domain's own vocabulary; (iii) any predicted reintegration states an observable consequence. Output failing (i)–(iii) is illustrative, not diagnostic.

**Corpus inversion rule (new in v4, sugg. 2).** A conjecture engine for RP3: *polarity without a known invariant → hunt the invariant; invariant without a known polarity → hunt the missing mode.*

**Seven-step method for any candidate structure:**
1. State the polar pair.
2. Identify the candidate identity.
3. Name the parameter of limitation.
4. Demand the invariant.
5. Check against established mathematics/physics (E5 grading). **Candidate-filter adjunct (v4, sugg. 3):** the framework ranks competing candidates by template cleanliness — a proposed symmetry/sector/solution that cannot present a clean identity / parameter / invariant structure scores lower. Filter, not forecast.
6. Convert to a falsifiable bridge claim with a named falsifier (E3).
7. Tag the tier, log it in the corpus (RP3); on failure — revise or drop, and record the failure.
