## III. Steelman

**S1 — The privation argument, strongest form.** (1) A privation is the absence of a due state, not a reality (blindness/sight, darkness/light, cold/heat). (2) Predicates of **pure perfection** are precisely those whose negations are privations. (3) Denying a privation affirms the due state; it excludes a *lack*, and excluding a lack is no limitation. (4) Therefore, for the full predicate-set the framework actually uses — complete, simple, perfect, without defects, without preference, self-sufficient — "A is not F" where F names a privation introduces no determination-loss; the first apophatic objection (exclusion) fails in its strongest form, not just in the file's weaker casualties. The one remaining condition: the license reaches **only** pure perfections — which is exactly the v6 criterion, now classically named.

**S2 — The language-gap, strongest form (three-gap taxonomy).** The limits are distinct and should not be conflated: (i) **expressibility gap** — P-present vs F-expressible/finite articulation (D10, extended: F-expressible rider); (ii) **tier gap** — true at display level, unformulable or non-exhaustive at ultimate level (D12); (iii) **medium gap** — language is a display-level structure (D1-family), and by A2/P2 it is *A appearing as language*; hence every gap is **modal within identity**, never ontological between two realities. Deep wiring available and now installed: statements about A are **formalities founded in A, not res** — the v6 formal distinction applied to predication itself. "Greater dissimilarity" (the Thomist cost of the analogical mode) is discharged structurally by D12's ultimate-unformulable clause rather than by apology. The second apophatic objection thereby reduces to: *"finite formalities reach A only as founded formalities"* — which is not an objection to the framework; it **is** the framework's own doctrine.

**S3 — The apophatic position, steelmanned as rival posture, not rival argument.** Strongest version: not "your predicates are wrong" but "metaphysics as predication ends in silence; silence is the honest terminus." This is a **rival commitment**, self-consistent as discipline, empty as refutation (it discriminates against nothing, since it exempts itself only by contradiction). The correct epistemic standing for v7: **acknowledged rival horizon; engaged where it argues; accommodated where it marks the boundary condition of the medium.**

**S4 — Kataphatic commitment as declared choice.** The file's demand ("the framework should say so") is right: v7 declares — the system is kataphatic by commitment in the rationalist lineage (Parmenides → Plotinus → Aquinas → Spinoza → Hegel), with the predication premise as its explicit charter; apophaticism stands as the acknowledged limit-perspective, not refuted, not adopted.
