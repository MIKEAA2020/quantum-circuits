# The Framework

## A Complete Formalization

---

## 0. Status

This is an axiom system. Given the axioms, the propositions follow necessarily. The axioms are metaphysical commitments, not theorems of neutral logic. The system is internally coherent, complete, elegant, and generative. It is a rationalist terminus.

---

## 1. Primitives

- **A** — the Absolute.
- **D** — appearance, the display.
- **M** — the totality of mathematical truth.
- **C** — consciousness, self-luminous awareness.
- **P** — polarity, the structure of one identity appearing as two modes.

---

## 2. Definitions

**D1 — Self-luminous.**
**A** knows itself by identity: **A** = **A**. Its self-knowing is not a relation between two things. It is the identity of knowing and being.

**D2 — Appearance.**
**D** is the self-display of **A**. It is not a second reality. It is **A** appearing as multiplicity.

**D3 — Mathematical.**
X is mathematical iff X has structure: identity, relation, distinction, order, symmetry, correspondence.

**D4 — Polarity.**
A structure is polar iff it has one identity, two modes, a parameter, and an invariant. The two modes are not separate realities; they are the identity viewed under two aspects.

**D5 — Parsimony.**
The generative principle of **D**: no unnecessary additions, no arbitrary exclusions. Parsimony is the character of the mapping from **A** to **D**. It is not an attribute of **A**; it is intrinsic to **A**'s self-expression.

**D6 — Virtuous circularity.**
The form of **A**'s self-identity. **A**'s self-knowledge is not a relation between knower and known. It is identity. Any account of this identity is circular because there is no external vantage point. The circularity is not a loop of dependence. It is the form of self-identity.

**D7 — No preference.**
**A** has no preference among structures, laws, or possibilities. This is a consequence of completeness (no lack) and beauty (no asymmetry).

**D8 — Plenitude.**
The principle that all consistent structures appear in **D**. This follows from no preference: no exclusion without preference.

## 3. Axioms

**A1 — Completeness.**
**A** lacks nothing.

**A2 — Non-duality.**
There is no ultimate reality other than **A**.

**A3 — Full intelligibility.**
There are no brute facts. Every feature of **D** is intelligible.

**A4 — Maximal beauty.**
**A** is maximally beautiful. Intelligibility is beautiful; mystery is a defect.

## 4. Derivation of **A**'s Attributes

**T1 — Simplicity.**
**A** has no parts.
*Proof.* If **A** had parts, it would depend on them. That violates self-existence (T2). Also, composition implies lack: the whole lacks the parts as separate, and the parts lack the whole. Contradiction with A1. Therefore **A** is simple. ∎

**T2 — Self-existence.**
**A** depends on nothing else.
*Proof.* If **A** depended on something else, it would lack self-sufficiency. Contradiction with A1. Therefore **A** is self-existent. ∎

**T3 — Necessity.**
**A** cannot be contingent.
*Proof.* If **A** were contingent, it would need a ground. That ground would be other than **A** (violates A2), or it would be **A** itself (self-grounded, which is necessity). Therefore **A** is necessary. ∎

**T4 — Immutability.**
**A** cannot change.
*Proof.* Change requires potential—the capacity to be otherwise. Potential implies lack (something not yet actual). Contradiction with A1. Therefore **A** is immutable. ∎

**T5 — Timelessness.**
**A** is not temporal.
*Proof.* Time implies succession, before and after, change. Contradiction with T4. Therefore **A** is timeless. ∎

**T6 — Spacelessness.**
**A** is not extended.
*Proof.* Extension implies parts, relation, distinction. Contradiction with T1 and A2. Therefore **A** is spaceless. ∎

**T7 — Relationlessness.**
**A** has no relations.
*Proof.* A relation requires two terms. If **A** related to something, there would be a second reality. Contradiction with A2. Therefore **A** is relationless. ∎

**T8 — No defects.**
**A** has no defects.
*Proof.* A defect is a lack. Contradiction with A1. Therefore **A** has no defects. ∎

**T9 — Self-luminosity.**
**A** is self-luminous.
*Proof.* If **A** lacked self-knowledge, it would lack something. Contradiction with A1. Therefore **A** is self-luminous. ∎

**T10 — Full intelligibility of A.**
**A** is fully intelligible.
*Proof.* **A** is complete. Unintelligibility is a lack of intelligibility. Contradiction with A1. Therefore **A** is fully intelligible. ∎

**T11 — Maximal beauty of A.**
**A** is maximally beautiful.
*Proof.* Beauty is a perfection. **A** is complete. Therefore **A** has maximal beauty. ∎

## 5. Derivation of Appearance

**Lemma 1 — Self-knowledge requires structure.**
Pure undifferentiated simplicity cannot be an object of knowledge. To know itself, **A** must have internal articulation: distinction, relation, order. This is not a lack; it is the form of self-luminosity.

**P1 — Appearance is necessary.**
*Proof.* **A** is self-luminous (T9). Self-knowledge requires structure (Lemma 1). Structure is mathematical (D3). Therefore **A**'s self-knowledge is the totality of mathematical truth (**M**). **M** is the web of all consistent structures. This web, viewed from within, is **D**. Therefore **D** is necessary. ∎

**P2 — Appearance is non-dually identical with A.**
*Proof.* **D** is **A**'s self-knowledge. It is not a second reality (A2). It is **A** appearing as multiplicity (D2). ∎

**P3 — Appearance is mathematical.**
*Proof.* **D** is **A**'s self-knowledge. Self-knowledge has structure. Structure is mathematical. Therefore **D** is mathematical. ∎

**P4 — Appearance is polar.**
*Proof.* The fundamental polarity is identity/appearance. Every genuine structure exhibits one identity, two modes, a parameter, an invariant (D4). **D** is structured (P3). Therefore **D** is polar. ∎

**P5 — Appearance is parsimonious.**
*Proof.* **A** has no preference (D7). No preference entails no arbitrary additions. The display unfolds according to parsimony (D5). ∎

**P6 — Appearance is self-similar.**
*Proof.* Parsimony plus polarity yields recursive, scale-invariant structure. ∎

**P7 — No brute facts in appearance.**
*Proof.* Every feature of **D** is intelligible (A3) and grounded in **A** (P2). ∎

**P8 — The specific form of D is deducible in principle from M.**
*Proof.* **M** is the totality of **A**'s self-knowledge. **D** is that totality laid out. Every feature of **D** is contained in **M**. Therefore every feature is deducible in principle. ∎
*Epistemic caveat.* Finite perspectives cannot perform this deduction. It is ontologically necessary, not epistemically accessible. Gödel shows no finite consistent system captures all of **M**.

**P9 — Plenitude.**
*Proof.* **A** has no preference (D7). No preference means no exclusion. No exclusion means all consistent structures appear. Therefore all consistent structures appear in **D**. ∎

**P10 — Consciousness is the ground.**
*Proof.* Consciousness is **A**'s self-luminosity (T9). It is not produced by non-conscious matter. It is the ground of **D**. ∎

**P11 — The hard problem is dissolved.**
*Proof.* Consciousness is not emergent from non-conscious matter. It is the ground. The question "how does matter produce consciousness?" is malformed because matter is appearance, not the ground. ∎

## 6. The Structure of Appearance

**P12 — Time, change, and process are appearance-level.**
*Proof.* **A** is timeless (T5), immutable (T4). **D** is **A**'s self-display. Therefore time, change, and process are structures within **D**, not properties of **A**. ∎

**P13 — The block universe is a valid description of D.**
*Proof.* **D** is the whole of **A**'s self-knowledge laid out. The whole is given at once. Time is a dimension within **D**, not a flow in **A**. Therefore the block universe is a display-level description. ∎

**P14 — Branching is the internal articulation of the block.**
*Proof.* **D** is the web of all consistent structures (P9). The web has branching structure: all possibilities are equally real. The block is the whole; branching is its internal structure. ∎

**P15 — Quantum indeterminism is symmetry.**
*Proof.* **A** has no preference (D7). No outcome is privileged. All outcomes are equally real (P14). Apparent randomness is the measure of this symmetry. The Born rule is the measure of the symmetry, not a fundamental stochastic law. ∎

**P16 — Physics discovers correlations, not ultimate causation.**
*Proof.* Causation is display-level. It is a structure within **D**. Grounding is non-dual (P2). Physics maps correlations within **D**. It does not discover ultimate causes. ∎

**P17 — The unified structure of physics is the mathematical web.**
*Proof.* **D** is mathematical (P3). The unified structure is a node in the web. Gauge fields and gravity are polar modes of one identity. The identity is the geometry of connections—or the spectral triple, or the holographic duality. ∎

## 8. The Geometry of Identity

The point–circle relation is the simplest expression:

\[
x^2 + y^2 = r^2
\]

- \(r = 0\): the point. **A**. No extension, no division, pure identity.
- \(r > 0\): the circle. **D**. Extension, relation, multiplicity.
- The equation is the identity: **A** = **A**.
- The radius is the parameter of limitation: the parsimonious mapping rule.

The circle is the point laid out. They are not two things. The point is the circle with zero radius. The circle is the point with non-zero radius. The equation does not change.
