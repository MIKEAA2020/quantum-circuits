Here is the clean distinction.

### 1\. Grounding is not correlation

- **Grounding:** The Absolute is the ground of all appearance. This is asymmetric, non-temporal, and non-statistical. Appearance depends on the Absolute; the Absolute depends on nothing.

- **Superdeterminism:** A specific claim about statistical correlations among physical variables. It says measurement settings and hidden variables are correlated in a way that violates statistical independence.


These are different categories. “Grounded in the Absolute” does not mean “all events are statistically correlated.” Correlation is a symmetric, display-level relation. Grounding is an asymmetric, ultimate relation. You cannot reduce one to the other.

### 2\. What superdeterminism actually says

Superdeterminism is not “everything is correlated.” It is a family of hidden-variable theories that preserve **locality and determinism** by rejecting **statistical independence**. In simple terms:

- Bell’s theorem says local hidden variables cannot reproduce quantum correlations unless measurement choices are correlated with the hidden variables.

- Superdeterminism accepts that correlation. It says the universe’s initial state is fine-tuned so that experimenters’ choices are not free in the relevant sense—they are determined by the same hidden variables.


So it is a **specific, deterministic, local hidden-variable hypothesis**. It is a loophole in Bell’s theorem, not a positive metaphysical picture. It requires a kind of cosmic conspiracy: the past is arranged so that measurement settings and hidden states line up. Many physicists find this unattractive.

### 3\. In your framework, all QM interpretations are appearance-level

Your Absolute is timeless, non-dual, relationless. Time, causality, determinism, indeterminism, hidden variables, measurement, collapse—all of these are **appearance**. They are patterns within the display. No QM interpretation is ultimately true. They are maps of the dream.

So the question “which QM interpretation is best?” is a question about the **structure of appearance**, not about the Absolute. You can choose one for pragmatic reasons—predictive power, simplicity, mathematical elegance—but none is metaphysically final. Superdeterminism is compatible with your framework, but so are relational QM, QBism, many-worlds, and Copenhagen. The Absolute does not pick one.

### 4\. Is superdeterminism the best?

No, not even at the display level. It has serious costs:

- It denies free choice of measurement settings in a strong sense.
