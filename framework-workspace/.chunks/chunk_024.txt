## 1\. What logic alone can tell us

**If process is absolutely unreal:**

- There is no change, no time, no becoming.

- All distinctions are illusion.

But to _know_ it, a process of understanding must occur. That is epistemic, not necessarily ontic—so it’s not a decisive contradiction. Still, it leaves appearance unexplained.
