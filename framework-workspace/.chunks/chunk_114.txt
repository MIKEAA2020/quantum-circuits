### What you are saying

Appearance is not optional. It is not a free, beginningless display that happens to be there. It is **necessary** to the Absolute's completeness. ### What it entails

If appearance is necessary to completeness, then:

1. **The Absolute is not complete without appearance.** It needs appearance to be what it is. This introduces a kind of dependence—not on something external, but on its own self-expression.

2. **Appearance is necessary, not contingent.** It could not have been otherwise. There must be appearance.

3. **The Absolute's simplicity is compromised—or reinterpreted.** If the Absolute's nature includes self-display, then it is not bare simplicity. It is a unity that includes its own manifestation.

4. **The Absolute is dynamic—or at least internally related to its display.** If it must appear, then there is a sense in which its being is tied to its appearing. This is not timeless immutability in the strict sense. It is a self-expressing completeness.

5. **Maya is no longer inexplicable.** Appearance is explained by the Absolute's need for completeness. It is not beginningless and arbitrary. It is necessary.
