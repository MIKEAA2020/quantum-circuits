# PART I — THE STRICT FORMALISM

## 0. Status

This is an **axiom system**. Given the axioms, the propositions follow necessarily; the axioms are metaphysical commitments, not theorems of neutral logic. The system is internally coherent and generative. It is a rationalist terminus.

Three honest declarations (v2, kept):

1. **Ontological gap.** The axioms define and commit to the Absolute; they do not prove its existence from neutral premises. Accepted with supporting arguments — not demonstrated such that refusal is irrational.
2. **"Complete" means no doctrinal remainder** — not formally complete (Gödel; see P8).
3. **No suchness remainder.** "Suchness of maya" and "not deducible from the Absolute alone" are retracted (chat turn 136). No brute facts in the display; the only boundary is **epistemic**.

Every proposition in Part I is tagged **[Ultimate]** or **[Appearance]**. Nothing in Part II is axiomatic; Part II inherits E2's tagging discipline but its contents are all regulative **[Regulative]**, **[Appearance-illustrative]**, or **[Comparative]**.

## 2. Definitions

**D1 — Self-luminous.** A knows itself by identity: A = A. Its self-knowing is not a relation between two things; it is the identity of knowing and being. *(Defines C.)*

**D2 — Appearance.** D is the self-display of A; not a second reality; A appearing as multiplicity.

**D3 — Mathematical.** X is mathematical iff X has structure: identity, relation, distinction, order, symmetry, correspondence.

**D4 — Polarity.** A structure is polar iff it has one identity, two modes, a parameter, and an invariant. The modes are not separate realities; they are the identity under two aspects. *(Defines P.)*

**D5 — Parsimony.** The generative principle of D: no unnecessary additions, no arbitrary exclusions; the character of the mapping A → D; not an attribute of A, but intrinsic to A's self-expression.

**D6 — Virtuous circularity.** The form of A's self-identity: self-knowledge is identity, not a knower/known relation; any account of it is circular because there is no external vantage point; not a loop of dependence.

**D7 — No preference.** A has no preference among structures, laws, or possibilities. *Justification:* a preference would require a selective reason; one outside A violates A2; one inside A that excludes a consistent structure would be an arbitrary boundary — a defect of intelligibility (T12, the A3-doctrine) and a partiality/lack (A1). Beauty (A4/T11) supports as maximal order but does not carry the load (J4).

**D8 — Plenitude.** All consistent structures appear in D; follows from no preference (no exclusion without preference) **together with the Expression Premise (L2, §5)** — v8: the inference from "no exclusion" to "full display" requires its own declared premise; it is not deducible from preference alone.

**D9 — Simplicity → Parsimony → Complexity.** Simplicity is an attribute of A (T1) — what A *is*. Parsimony is the character of the intrinsic mapping rules (D5) — *how* simplicity expresses as structure. Complexity is the display — the articulated *result*. Complexity does not conflict with parsimony; it is produced by it.

**D10 — Two senses of "deducible."**
- **F-deducible** (finite): derivable by finite reasoning in a finite formal system. *Scope correction (v8):* Gödel's theorems show that no single consistent, effectively axiomatized, sufficiently expressive system proves all arithmetic truths; they do **not** by themselves show that particular features of D (the constants, the gauge group) are underivable. The boundary this system asserts is therefore **metaphysical, Gödel-resonant, not Gödel-derived**; claiming specific underivability is an additional (risky, display-level) claim, not a consequence of D10 — see J6's reframing as a bet.
- **P-present** (present-in-knowledge): contained in A's complete self-knowledge — belonging to M as a necessary truth of the whole.
"Deducible in principle" always means P-present, never F-deducible (J5).
- **M's two-tier status (v6 canonical sentence).** M is **complete qua P-present** in A's self-knowledge, and **indefinitely extensible qua F-schema** in any finite formalization. "Schema" marks our access, never a lack in A (D12: the word "M" itself obeys the two-truths rule). This discharges the J3 tension between P1 (self-knowledge **is** M) and A1 (no lack).

**D11 — Three senses of "real." (New in v3 — from the chat's process dialectic, S9.)**
- **R1, real-as-actual:** actual *within* the display (a stone falls; a manuscript is revised; experiences occur).
- **R2, real-as-ultimate:** belonging to the ground — to A itself.
- **R3, real-in-the-strongest-sense:** that which is, context-free. Only A.
*Usage rule:* unqualified "real" means R3. Most disputes about process, change, time, and perfection are R1-vs-R3 vocabulary collisions; naming the sense dissolves them.

**D12 — Perspective and two truths. (New in v3 — S26, S66.)**
- A **perspective** is a localized view within D. Localization is not a brute accident: completeness → self-knowledge → structure → web of correspondences → perspectives (a rational reconstruction of why perspectives exist — persuasive, flagged as reconstruction, not demonstration).
- A is **transjective**: beyond the subject/object split, which is display-internal. Conventional "objective" and "subjective" both live inside D.
- **Two-truths rule:** a statement may be true at display level and false or unformulable at ultimate level; the tier must always be named.

## 3. Axioms

- **A1 — Completeness.** A lacks nothing. **[Ultimate]**
- **A2 — Non-duality.** There is no ultimate reality other than A. **[Ultimate]**
- **A3 — Full intelligibility. *(Restated as theorem T12 in §4 — v8; kept here for the audit trail.)*** No brute facts; every feature of D is intelligible. Doctrine-driving (P7, P8). **[Ultimate]**

**The Premise of Perfection-Predication (v6 license, renamed and extended v7).** T1–T11 and A4 jointly presuppose one rule: *"X is a perfection + A1 ⇒ A has X."* It is now a **declared premise** — a truth-apt claim, not a permission — stated in its classical form and with its warrant:

1. **Scope: pure perfections only.** The premise reaches exactly the *perfectiones simpliciter* (Aquinas) — attributes signifying no admixture of imperfection, i.e., attributes whose **absence would constitute an A1/A3-violating lack** (the v6 criterion, now identified as the Thomist filter). **Mixed perfections are not licensed**: rationality, locality, temporality, personality-in-the-anthropic-sense, and every attribute whose absence is not a lack do not pass; nothing in Part I predicates them essentially.
2. **Warrant: the privation argument.** A privation is the absence of a due state, not a reality (blindness/sight; darkness/light; cold/heat). The predicates Part I actually uses — complete, simple, perfect, without defects, without preference, self-sufficient — are pure perfections: their negations are privations. **Denying a privation affirms the due state; it excludes a lack, and excluding a lack is not a limitation.** Hence the first apophatic objection (every predicate excludes, therefore limits) fails for this predicate-set in its strongest form — determinatio est negatio (Spinoza's principle, later Hegel's "bad infinity" critique) does not touch negations of privations.
3. **Mode: analogical, kataphatic, declared.** Predicates apply to A in the analogical mode (likeness founded; dissimilarity greater). The greater dissimilarity is not apologized away; it is discharged structurally by D12 (true at display level, unformulable-as-exhaustive at ultimate level) and by the v6 formal distinction: predicates of A are **formalities founded in A, not res** — the license is Lemma 1's v6 criterion applied to predication itself. The framework is **kataphatic by declared commitment** (Parmenides → Plotinus → Aquinas → Spinoza → Hegel); apophaticism is the acknowledged rival horizon (J8), not refuted, not adopted.

Under this premise, beauty qualifies insofar as beauty = maximal order/intelligibility — hence (i) A4's metaphysical content **overlaps A3**; overlap declared, not hidden. Its independent remainder — "mystery is a defect" as a value-inflection — is **[aesthetic-regulative]** work (Wheeler alignment, RP6), never inference: (ii) **beauty ⇒ truth is not licensed by this system** (RP7). (iii) A4 is **kept as axiom**: demotion is a pure loss (T11 re-derives beauty from A1 under the premise; the premise is needed by A1/T8/D7 regardless, so demotion buys no apophatic safety either — both points now on record against the 2026-10-10 audit's table); promotion beyond attribute-status is unearned. See J4, J8.

## 5. The articulation premise and the derivation of appearance

**Lemma 1 — Self-knowledge requires structure (defended).** Pure undifferentiated simplicity cannot be an object of knowledge; self-knowledge requires internal articulation. *Defense:* (i) articulation is **aspectual, not partitive** — a part can exist apart, an aspect cannot; T1 denies parts, not aspects; (ii) otherwise either A lacks self-knowledge (against T9) or "knowledge without anything structured to be known" is empty (against D1); (iii) articulation is not external relation (T7) — it is self-relation, i.e., identity (D6). *Honest residue:* that aspectual articulation does not compromise simplicity is the classical non-dual move; coherent, necessary, and the exact joint an adversary presses (J1).

**The criterion sharpened (v6 — formal distinction).** An *aspect* is a **formal distinction with foundation in reality and without addition of reality** (scholastic *distinctio rationis ratiocinatae*): (i) **non-partitive** — aspects cannot exist apart, and the fusion of A with its aspects is A, not A+1 (no addition of being); (ii) **non-relational** — the relata are *formalities*, not *res*: there is no second term that is a reality (T7 safe), no independently subsisting unit (T1 safe), and the formalities are numerically identical *in re* (D6 safe: self-"relation" is identity because the relata were never two beings); (iii) **sufficient** — self-knowledge requires only internal *contrast*, not *otherness*; otherness is what vantages add (D12). This dissolves the audit's J1 dilemma between "real distinction" (breaks T7) and "mere display-distinction" (demotes Lemma 1): the third category stands between *res* and *ratio*. Comparative anchors [Comparative]: Aquinas's divine attributes (distinct *secundum rationem*, identical *in re*); Advaita *svaprakāśa* (self-luminosity without self-objectification — T9's anchor); *sat-chit-ananda* as the paradigm formal triad.

**Status decision (v6, strengthened v8).** Lemma 1 **remains a defended premise**, now with the sharp criterion above. *v8 — premise-class note:* the system's non-axiomatic declaratives are four, not "one": **L1** (this lemma), the **Predication Premise** (§3), **L2** (below), and **D3's scope** (qualitative content is structure) — the J1 registry line records this explicitly.
