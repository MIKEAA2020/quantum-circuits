## 0. Status

This is an **axiom system**. Given the axioms, the propositions follow necessarily; the axioms are metaphysical commitments, not theorems of neutral logic. The system is internally coherent and generative. It is a rationalist terminus.

Three honest declarations, tightened in v2:

1. **Ontological gap.** The axioms *define and commit to* the Absolute; they do not prove its existence from neutral premises (a definition of "perfect" cannot manufacture the perfect). The system is a framework one *accepts*, with its supporting arguments — not a demonstration one cannot refuse.
2. **"Complete" means no doctrinal remainder.** It does **not** mean formally complete: no formal system of this richness is complete (Gödel), and the system says this about itself in P8. Completeness here is a claim about the ground, not about the axiom system's proof power.
3. **No suchness remainder.** The earlier hedges — "suchness of maya," "not deducible from the Absolute alone" — are retracted (turn 136). There are no brute facts in the display. The only boundary is **epistemic**: finite perspectives cannot survey the totality. Reality has no gaps; knowledge does.

Every proposition is tagged **[Ultimate]** or **[Appearance]**. The two tiers never mix (E2).

## 2. Definitions

**D1 — Self-luminous.** A knows itself by identity: A = A. Its self-knowing is not a relation between two things; it is the identity of knowing and being. *(This also defines C: consciousness is A's self-luminosity.)*

**D2 — Appearance.** D is the self-display of A. It is not a second reality. It is A appearing as multiplicity.

**D3 — Mathematical.** X is mathematical iff X has structure: identity, relation, distinction, order, symmetry, correspondence.

**D4 — Polarity.** A structure is polar iff it has one identity, two modes, a parameter, and an invariant. The two modes are not separate realities; they are the identity viewed under two aspects. *(This defines P.)*

**D5 — Parsimony.** The generative principle of D: no unnecessary additions, no arbitrary exclusions. Parsimony is the character of the mapping from A to D. It is not an attribute of A; it is intrinsic to A's self-expression.

**D6 — Virtuous circularity.** The form of A's self-identity. A's self-knowledge is not a relation between knower and known; it is identity. Any account of it is circular because there is no external vantage point. The circularity is not a loop of dependence; it is the form of self-identity.

**D7 — No preference. (Re-grounded in v2.)** A has no preference among structures, laws, or possibilities. *Justification:* a preference would require a selective reason. A selective reason not contained in A would be an external ground (against A2). A selective reason within A, excluding some consistent structure, would be an arbitrary boundary — a defect of intelligibility (against A3) and a partiality, hence a lack (against A1). Beauty (A4/T11) *supports* this as maximal order, but the load is carried by A1 + A2 + A3. *(v2 fix: the earlier "beauty (no asymmetry)" leg is demoted from load-bearing to supporting — see §12, J4.)*

**D8 — Plenitude.** All consistent structures appear in D. This follows from no preference: no exclusion without preference.

**D9 — Simplicity → Parsimony → Complexity. (New in v2, from audit 1 / A2.)** Simplicity is an *attribute of A* (T1) — what A **is**. Parsimony is the *character of the intrinsic mapping rules* A → D (D5) — **how** A's simplicity expresses itself as structure. Complexity is the *display* — the articulated **result** of applying the parsimonious mapping rules. Complexity does not conflict with parsimony; it is produced by it.

**D10 — Two senses of "deducible." (New in v2, resolves the containment/deduction equivocation.)**

- **F-deducible** (finite): derivable by a finite chain of reasoning within a finite formal system. *Nothing* of this richness is F-deducible by finite perspectives (Gödel).
- **P-present** (present-in-knowledge): contained in A's complete self-knowledge — i.e., belonging to M as a necessary truth of the whole.

"X is deducible in principle" shall always mean **P-present**, never F-deducible. The containment of a feature in M is *presence*, not *derivation*; the system claims the first, not the second.

## 4. Derivation of A's attributes

- **T1 — Simplicity.** If A had parts, it would depend on them (against self-existence, T2); and composition implies lack — the whole lacks the parts as separate, the parts lack the whole — against A1. Hence A has no parts. ∎
- **T2 — Self-existence.** If A depended on something else, it would lack self-sufficiency — against A1. Hence A depends on nothing else. ∎
- **T3 — Necessity.** If A were contingent, it would need a ground — other than A (against A2), or A itself (self-grounded necessity). Hence A cannot be contingent. ∎
- **T4 — Immutability.** Change requires potential — the capacity to be otherwise. Potential implies lack: something not yet actual. Against A1. Hence A cannot change. ∎
- **T5 — Timelessness.** Time implies succession, before and after, change. Against T4. Hence A is not temporal. ∎
- **T6 — Spacelessness.** Extension implies parts, relation, distinction. Against T1 and A2. Hence A is not extended. ∎
- **T7 — Relationlessness.** A relation requires two terms. If A related to something, there would be a second reality. Against A2. Hence A has no external relations. *(Internal articulation is identity, not relation — see Lemma 1.)* ∎
- **T8 — No defects.** A defect is a lack. Against A1. Hence A has no defects. ∎
- **T9 — Self-luminosity.** If A lacked self-knowledge, it would lack something. Against A1. Hence A is self-luminous. *(This yields C as derived, per §1.)* ∎
- **T10 — Full intelligibility of A.** Unintelligibility is a lack of intelligibility. Against A1. Hence A is fully intelligible. *(The A-side twin of A3; kept for symmetry.)* ∎
- **T11 — Maximal beauty of A.** Beauty is a perfection; A is complete (A1); hence A has maximal beauty. *(Redundancy pair with A4 — see §3 note. The weakest derivation in the system; an objector need not accept "beauty is a perfection," and nothing load-bearing depends on it after the v2 regrounding of D7.)* ∎

## 5. The articulation premise and the derivation of appearance

**Lemma 1 — Self-knowledge requires structure. (Defended in v2.)**

Pure undifferentiated simplicity cannot be an object of knowledge. To know itself, A must have internal articulation: distinction, relation, order. *(v1 asserted this without defense; it is the one premise not forceable from A1–A4 alone. Defense:)*

- **Articulation is not composition.** A *part* can exist apart from the whole; an *aspect* cannot. Internal articulation means self-knowledge has distinguishable **aspects** (adjectival differences), not separable **parts**. T1 denies parts, not aspects. A map of a territory has structure without the territory being assembled from detached pieces.
- **The alternatives are worse.** Either A has no self-knowledge (against T9), or "self-knowledge with no articulation" is an empty label — knowledge with nothing known, no structure to be known — which is not knowledge at all (against D1). So self-knowledge requires articulation.
- **Articulation is not external relation.** T7 bars relation *to an other*. Internal articulation is self-relation — which, for A, is identity (D6), not a two-term bond.
- **Honest residue.** That aspectual articulation does not compromise simplicity is the classical non-dual move (internal difference without real division). It is coherent, it is necessary for the system, and it is the exact joint an adversarial reader will press. It is declared, not disguised. (Registry J1.)

From the premise:

- **P1 — Appearance is necessary. [Ultimate]** A is self-luminous (T9); self-knowledge requires structure (Lemma 1); structure is mathematical (D3); hence A's self-knowledge is M. M — the web of all consistent structures — viewed from within, is D. Hence D is necessary. ∎
- **P2 — Appearance is non-dually identical with A. [Ultimate]** D is A's self-knowledge — not a second reality (A2) — A appearing as multiplicity (D2). ∎
- **P3 — Appearance is mathematical. [Ultimate]** D has structure (self-knowledge); structure is mathematical (D3). ∎
- **P4 — Appearance is polar. [Appearance]** The fundamental polarity is identity/appearance; every genuine structure exhibits one identity, two modes, a parameter, an invariant (D4); D is structured (P3). ∎
- **P5 — Appearance is parsimonious. [Appearance]** A has no preference (D7) ⇒ no arbitrary additions; D unfolds according to D5. ∎
- **P6 — Appearance is self-similar. [Appearance]** Parsimony plus polarity yields recursive, scale-invariant structure. ∎
- **P7 — No brute facts in appearance. [Ultimate]** Every feature of D is intelligible (A3) and grounded in A (P2). ∎
- **P8 — The specific form of D is P-present in M. (Rewritten in v2.) [Ultimate]** M is the totality of A's self-knowledge; D is that totality laid out; every feature of D is present in M — ontologically necessary, not arbitrary. No feature of D is F-deducible by finite perspectives (Gödel; D10). *The boundary is in us, not in reality.* ∎
- **P9 — Plenitude. [Appearance]** A has no preference (D7); no preference means no exclusion; hence all consistent structures appear in D. ∎ *(Inherits D7's disclosed supporting structure: A1 + A2 + A3, beauty supporting.)*
- **P10 — Consciousness is the ground. [Ultimate]** Consciousness is A's self-luminosity (T9); it is not produced by non-conscious matter; it is the ground of D. ∎
- **P11 — The hard problem is dissolved. [Ultimate]** Consciousness is not emergent from non-conscious matter; matter is appearance, not the ground; "how does matter produce consciousness?" is malformed. ∎

## 6. The structure of appearance

- **P12 — Time, change, and process are appearance-level. [Appearance]** A is timeless (T5) and immutable (T4); D is A's self-display; hence time, change, and process are structures within D, not properties of A. ∎
- **P13 — The block universe is a valid description of D. [Appearance]** D is the whole of A's self-knowledge, given at once; time is a dimension within D, not a flow in A. ∎
- **P14 — Branching is the internal articulation of the block. [Appearance]** D is the web of all consistent structures (P9); all possibilities are equally real; the block is the whole; branching is its internal structure. ∎
- **P15 — Quantum indeterminism is symmetry. (Qualified in v2.) [Appearance]** A has no preference (D7) ⇒ no outcome privileged ⇒ all outcomes equally real (P14). Apparent randomness is the localized view of this symmetry; the Born rule is the *measure of the symmetry*, not a fundamental stochastic law. *Qualification (S71): this selects branching-block as the framework's **default display-grammar** — the best-aligned with no-preference — and not as the unique ultimate reading. Other interpretations remain legitimate maps. And since it makes no novel empirical predictions, it belongs to grammar, not to physics: claimed physics is F-falsifiable; this is not, so it must not be sold as a bridge claim.* ∎
- **P16 — Physics discovers correlations, not ultimate causation. [Appearance]** Causation is display-level structure within D; grounding is non-dual (P2); physics maps correlations within D. ∎
- **P17 — The unified structure of physics is the mathematical web. [Appearance]** D is mathematical (P3); the unified structure is a node in the web; gauge fields and gravity are polar modes of one identity — the geometry of connections, or the spectral triple, or the holographic duality. The framework predicts the *form*; it does not name the node. ∎
- **P18 — The Big Bang is the point–circle transition. (Audit 1 / B3.) [Appearance]** r = 0 is the singularity/point; r > 0 is expansion. The Big Bang is not an absolute beginning; it is a structural feature of the display. "Unique event or cycle?" presupposes time, which is appearance — malformed at the ground. Plenitude (P9) entails infinitely many cosmic histories, co-present in the timeless display. ∎
- **P19 — Physical polarities instantiate the template. (Audit 1 / B4.) [Appearance]** Electric charge (+/−), magnetic poles (N/S — no isolated pole: the sharpest physical image of non-duality), particle/antiparticle, wave/particle, fermion/boson. Each: one identity, two modes, a parameter, an invariant. ∎
- **P20 — The display is the mathematical web. (Audit 1 / B5.) [Appearance]** Category theory, group theory, representation theory, algebraic geometry, Langlands, AQFT, twistor theory, sacred geometry are modes of one identity; physics is a scale of the web. The framework predicts the *form* (polarity, correspondence, self-similarity), not the specific Lagrangian. ∎
- **P21 — Research heuristic. (Audit 1 / B6.) [Appearance, regulative]** Wherever a polarity is found, look for the identity, the parameter, and the invariant; hunt dualities, maximal symmetry plus breaking, and conserved (Noether-style) invariants. This is the generative principle for elaborating appearance without importing rival metaphysics. ∎

## 8. The geometry of identity

The point–circle relation is the simplest expression:

\[
x^2 + y^2 = r^2
\]

- \(r = 0\): the point. A. No extension, no division, pure identity.
- \(r > 0\): the circle. D. Extension, relation, multiplicity.
- The equation is the identity: A = A.
- The radius is the parameter of limitation: the parsimonious mapping rule.

The circle is the point laid out. They are not two things. The point is the circle with zero radius. The circle is the point with non-zero radius. The equation does not change.

## 10. Presentation constitution (new in v2 — the external audit's epistemics, codified)

- **E1 — Axiom system, not proof.** The framework is presented as a commitment with supporting arguments. The ontological gap (definition ≠ existence) is declared with it, always.
- **E2 — Two-tier tagging.** Every statement is tagged **[Ultimate]** or **[Appearance]**. Nothing migrates between tiers. Ultimate claims are untestable by design and are never presented as physics.
- **E3 — Lighthouse discipline.** The framework is regulative, not constitutive, for physics. Any **bridge claim** (a heuristic meant to steer empirical research) must state in advance what would falsify it. Physics retains a veto over bridge claims — if a bridge fails, the bridge is revised or dropped; the ground is untouched, but guidance in that direction ends.
- **E4 — No category-error transfers.** Absolute-attributes must not be read into formal structures (e.g., "Born rule ↔ self-existence," "no-go theorem ↔ no external vantage point" are out). Appearance does not "echo" the ground attribute-by-attribute; metaphysics is non-load-bearing in any derivation.
- **E5 — Evidence grading.** Established (mainstream) vs. contested vs. speculative must be labeled. Geometric Langlands / Kapustin–Witten / amplituhedron / AdS–CFT / Noether / Gödel = established. Prime–consciousness links, sacred-geometry "proofs," and non-peer-reviewed resonance preprints = speculative illustrations only, never evidence.
- **E6 — No self-validation moves.** No supertautology: the system's coherence is not evidence of its truth. Coherence is a property of the system; correspondence is the open question (E1).
- **E7 — Translation rules for imports.** Any concept imported from another framework must (i) be prefixed "at the level of appearance," (ii) be de-temporalized if applied to A, (iii) have its causal language read descriptively, not productively, (iv) pass the rigor test — extract structural pattern, strip ultimacy/becoming, restate as a falsifiable appearance-level claim, tag it, drop what fails. Importing concepts with their native axioms attached is a category error.

## 12. Open joints (declared registry)

These are the exact places an adversarial reader will press. They are stated openly rather than smoothed over.

- **J1 — Lemma 1.** The aspectual-vs-partitive distinction is the system's one non-axiomatic premise. Defense written (§5); residual exposure acknowledged.
- **J2 — The ontological gap.** Existence of A is not derivable from the axioms' content. The system is accept-with-argument, not prove-and-compel (E1).
- **J3 — The status of M.** "Totality of mathematical truth" resists objectification (Gödel/Tarski/indefinite extensibility). Treated as a schema (§1). If a stricter formalization is ever attempted, P1/P8/'s quantification over M is where care is needed — the doctrine (A3) does not depend on the set-theoretic reading.
- **J4 — A4/T11/D7.** Beauty is perfection-predication; plenitude now runs on A1 + A2 + A3 with beauty only supporting (D7). If an objector rejects even that, D7's no-preference is the fallback commitment — stated, used, owned.
- **J5 — P-present is not F-deducibility.** The doctrine says every display-feature is necessary; it does not give anyone the derivation. Any presentation implying otherwise violates D10 and E1.
- **J6 — Plenitude's specific form.** *Why* the branching/Hilbert-space form of possibilities, and why these constants, is at the Gödel horizon (P8) — open as knowledge, closed as doctrine.
- **J7 — Baseline empirical caution.** Physics alone does not decide timelessness vs. passage (stalemate); QM-as-symmetry carries no novel predictions; the framework's empirical value is heuristic (P21) under lighthouse rules (E3).
