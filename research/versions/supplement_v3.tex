This Supplemental Material collects the reference data behind Sec.~II.F and Appendix~A of the main text: the exceptional sets $E_L$ of the three-replica compressed operator for $d=2$ and $d=3$ with blocks, minimal-polynomial degrees and exact ranks (Sec.~\ref{sm:E}), the per-block certificate data at $L=12$ (Sec.~\ref{sm:L12}), the endpoint and $p_\times$ ranks (Sec.~\ref{sm:endpoints}), and the complete list of certificate files and scripts with SHA-256 hashes and reproduction commands (Sec.~\ref{sm:files}).  Every number is exact (integer, modular with the stated prime, or algebraic with a certified isolating interval); no floating-point computation enters.  Notation follows the main text: $\mathsf B_p$ is the integer-polynomial matrix of Eq.~(A1), $\Gamma=H\times\mathbb Z_m$ with $H=(S_3\times S_3)\rtimes\mathbb Z_2$, blocks are labelled $(\rho,k)$ by an irreducible representation $\rho$ of $H$ (dimension $d_\rho$; $\iota\pm$ is the transposition parity, ${\rm Ind}$ an induced representation of dimension $2$ or $4$) and a momentum $k$ of $\mathbb Z_m$ ($k=\pm1$ and $k=\pm2$ denote the rational block that is the sum of the two conjugate complex sectors).

\section{Exceptional sets}
\label{sm:E}

At a point $p^*\in E_L$ the drop of $\rk\mathsf B_{p^*}$ below the generic rank is $\sum d_\rho\,\nu_\rho$, where $\nu_\rho$ is the nullity of the block at $p^*$; the tables list, for each $p^*$, the block(s) whose invariant factor vanishes, the degree of the minimal polynomial $f$ of $p^*$ over $\mathbb Q$, the nullity, and the exact rank of $\mathsf B_{p^*}$.  The numerical value of $p^*$ is a truncation of the certified isolating interval (all intervals have width below $10^{-15}$).  For $d=2$ every interior zero is simple (certificate multiplicity equal to the nullity in all $11$ block factors at $L=8$ and the one at $L=6$); for $d=3$ all are simple except the linear factor $3p-2$ ($p_\times(3)=2/3$) in the $k=\pm1$ blocks $({\rm triv}\otimes{\rm triv})^{\iota+}$ at $L=6$, $({\rm std}\otimes{\rm std})^{\iota+}$ at $L=6,8$ and $({\rm sgn}\otimes{\rm sgn})^{\iota+}$ at $L=8$ (file \texttt{n3\_simple\_zero\_check.log}).

\section{Files, hashes and reproduction}
\label{sm:files}

All certificate files are plain JSON or text.  Table~\ref{tab:S7} lists them ($63$ files) with their full SHA-256 hashes; the same list, in \texttt{sha256sum} format, is the ledger \texttt{certificate\_sha256.txt}, so that \texttt{sha256sum -c certificate\_sha256.txt} verifies the deposit.  The later deposit rounds extend the ledger without altering any entry above: \texttt{certificate\_sha256\_v5.txt} (the $n=4$ colour-resolved scans, the first $n=5$ diagnostics, and the Haar sequential-Monte-Carlo results quoted in the main text) and \texttt{certificate\_sha256\_v6.txt} (the $n=5$ two-size test, the $n=4$ $L=10$ third crossing, and the no-freezing-theorem verification), each self-verifying in the same way.  Table~\ref{tab:S8} lists the scripts (Python~3, dependencies \texttt{numpy} and \texttt{python-flint}; \texttt{sympy} for the $d=2$ number-field ranks).  The reproduction sequence is given in Table~\ref{tab:S9}.
