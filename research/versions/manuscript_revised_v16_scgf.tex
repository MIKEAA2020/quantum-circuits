\title{Measurement-induced phase transition in random hybrid Clifford circuits:
exact annealed criticality and a high-precision quenched locator}
\author{}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
We study the measurement-induced entanglement transition in random hybrid
circuits of $L$ qudits (local dimension $d$) on a ring: two layers of random
two-site Clifford gates per period, each followed by independent
computational-basis measurements at rate $p$.  For the \emph{annealed}
(disorder-averaged) two-replica problem the full moment dynamics closes on a
three-dimensional per-site commutative algebra and is exactly similar to the
row transfer of an anisotropic triangular-lattice Ising model,
$\Ccomp=\Wz^L E D_h E^{\mathsf T}D_h$ acting on $2^{L/2}$ states, with
$K_d=\tfrac14\ln(a/c)$, $K_h=\tfrac14\ln(ac/b^2)$, $\Wz=(acb^2)^{1/4}$
determined by the measurement channel.  We prove a closed form for the
complete nonzero spectrum (a free-fermion product over parity-quantised
momenta, recovering the rank $3\cdot2^{L/2-2}$ by mode counting), and obtain
the exact annealed critical line
$\pctwo(d)=\bigl(d-\vth_c\bigr)/(d-1)$, $\vth_c=\beta_d+\sqrt{\beta_d^2+1}$,
$\beta_d=(d^2-1)/(d^2+1)$, with certified leading eigenvalues (exact-rational
Collatz--Wielandt enclosures of relative width $\le7.3\times10^{-15}$).
The record-collision weight of the annealed channel favours trajectories with
fewer measurements, lowering the effective monitoring density to
$p_{\rm eff}=p+\tfrac{p(1-p)}{2L}\partial_p\ln\lambda_1$; this resolves the
annealed-versus-quenched entropy ordering without a convexity argument.
For the \emph{quenched} Clifford dynamics we locate the transition from
$475{,}600$ trajectories ($L=16$--$512$) of the tripartite information
$I_3$ and the logarithmic entanglement entropy: crossings, windowed data
collapse, bootstrap errors): $p_c=0.1597(8)$, $\nu=1.24(7)$, and critical
half-chain entropy $\alpha=1.55(7)$ bits per $\ln L$ (systematic rather than
statistical uncertainties).  An independent purification locator
(Gullans--Huse reference-qubit protocol, disjoint seeds, mixed initial state,
$135{,}000$ trajectories) gives $p_c^{\rm purif}=0.1601$--$0.1604$,
$\nu^{\rm purif}=1.25(2)$.  Our values agree with Gullans--Huse
$0.1593(5)$, $\nu=1.28(2)$ and Sierant et al.\ $0.15995(10)$.
Finally, the record statistics admit a quenched scaled cumulant generating
function whose $\beta=1$ endpoint is exactly the two-replica partition
function: we verify its thermodynamic limit (the $\beta\to1$ side converging
to the Houtappel free energy per site, the quenched information density to
$0.1489$ bits per measurement site with an $L$-independent per-site
variance), demonstrate the freezing of the collision tilt (carried by
$O(1)$--$O(10)$ records at $L=8$--$24$), and show that the
annealed-minus-quenched free-energy gap per site,
$0.0222(5)$\,nats, is $L$-independent: the record measure is a glass whose
observables nevertheless self-average.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

Hybrid random circuits---interleaving unitary evolution with projective
measurements---display a measurement-induced entanglement transition between
a volume-law and an area-law phase
\citep{Skinner2019,Li2018,Gullans2020,Nahum2020}.  Numerically the transition
is located through disorder-averaged observables ($I_3$, entropies,
purification) whose sample-to-sample fluctuations grow exponentially with
system size; in monitored \emph{Clifford} circuits those averages can be
sampled exactly by stabilizer simulation, which makes the Clifford ensemble
the natural high-precision testbed \citep{Gullans2020,Sierant2022,Zabalo2020}.

This paper combines two complementary attacks on the same Clifford model.
(i)~\emph{Exact annealed results.}  The second moment of the monitored
channel averaged over circuits (equivalently over measurement \emph{records}
with the record-collision weight $P(R)^2$) is exactly solvable: the
two-replica dynamics closes on a per-site algebra of three commuting
projectors, compresses to a classical transfer matrix of Ising type, and
yields the full finite-size spectrum in closed form, the exact annealed
critical line for every local dimension $d$, and certified eigenvalues.
(ii)~\emph{Quenched numerics.}  A high-precision stabilizer simulation of the
\emph{Born-sampled} (quenched) dynamics pins the transition at
$p_c=0.1597(8)$ with $\nu=1.24(7)$ from $475{,}600$ trajectories, and an
independent Gullans--Huse purification protocol with disjoint seeds confirms
the point through a different observable.

The paper is organised as follows.  Sec.~\ref{sec:model} defines the circuit
and the two-replica (record-collision) moment problem, introduces the
per-site algebra and the compressed classical transfer
(Eqs.~\ref{eq:M1M2}--\ref{eq:Kdh}), and states the rank theorem.
Sec.~\ref{sec:ising} identifies the classical model as the anisotropic

(Prop.~\ref{prop:houtappel}), and proves the closed-form spectrum
(Prop.~\ref{prop:spectrum}) together with its numerical certificate.
Sec.~\ref{sec:benchmark} verifies the exact solution against the

eigenvalues (App.~\ref{app:enclosures}).  Sec.~\ref{sec:annealed} derives the
exact annealed critical line and the effective-density (tilt) mechanism.
Sec.~\ref{sec:records} states the multifractal record statistics.
Sec.~\ref{sec:scgf} closes the record-level theorem items: the quenched

Sec.~\ref{sec:quenched} presents the quenched locator and the adopted

\section{Model, two-replica compression, and the rank theorem}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{The monitored Clifford circuit}
\label{sec:circuit}

The system is a ring of $L=2m$ qudits (dimension $d$, computational basis
$\{|x\rangle\}_{x=0}^{d-1}$).  One circuit \emph{period} consists of two
brickwork layers of random two-qudit gates (even bonds $(2k,2k+1)$, then odd
bonds $(2k+1,2k+2)$, each gate drawn independently and uniformly from the
two-qudit Clifford group), each gate layer followed by a measurement layer in
which every site is independently measured in the computational basis with
probability $p$.  A \emph{record} $R$ is the complete list of measurement
positions and outcomes along a trajectory; its Born probability under a
random circuit realisation is $P(R)$.  The quenched observables of interest
($I_3$, half-chain entropy $S_{L/2}$) are Born-sampled averages over
trajectories; the annealed second moments average the record probability
squared, which glues two replicas onto the same record.

\subsection{The record-collision moment and the per-site algebra}
\label{sec:replica}

The two-replica state $\sigma$ lives on
$(\mathbb{C}^d)^{\otimes L}\otimes(\mathbb{C}^d)^{\otimes L}$.  Under a gate
$U$ acting identically on both replicas, $\sigma\mapsto(U\otimes
U)\,\sigma\,(U^\dagger\otimes U^\dagger)$; since the two-qudit Clifford group
is an exact $2$-design, the circuit average of this conjugation is the
twirl onto the commutant $\operatorname{span}\{I,S\}$ of each gate bond ($S$
the replica swap).  A record-colliding measurement of site $i$ maps
$\sigma\mapsto(\Pi_i\otimes\Pi_i)\,\sigma\,(\Pi_i\otimes\Pi_i)$ with
$\Pi_i=\sum_x|x\rangle\langle x|$ the same-outcome projector on both
replicas.  The relevant operator algebra is therefore generated per site by
\begin{equation}
  e_0=\Pi,\qquad e_+=\tfrac{I+S}{2}-\Pi,\qquad e_-=\tfrac{I-S}{2},
  \label{eq:Vdef}
\end{equation}
three mutually orthogonal projectors ($e_0+e_++e_-=I$;
$Se_0=e_0,\ Se_+=e_+,\ Se_-=-e_-$).  The gate twirl preserves this algebra
and the colliding measurement acts diagonally on it,
$e_0\mapsto e_0$, $e_\pm\mapsto(1-p)\,e_\pm$; the whole annealed dynamics is
a positive linear map on the $3^L$-dimensional commutative algebra spanned
by the products $\bigotimes_k e_{a_k}$.  Because the transfer is
similar (Schur complement on the collided sector) to a $2^m$-dimensional
classical transfer acting on the pair variables, all annealed $n=2$
quantities coincide with the Haar ones by the $2$-design property
(Remark~\ref{rem:clifford}).

\subsection{The compressed classical transfer}
\label{sec:compression}

Let $s=(s_1,\dots,s_m)\in\{\pm1\}^m$ label the pair (row) configuration.
The half-period transfer is the $2^m\times2^m$ matrix
\begin{equation}
  M_1=\Wz^m\,E\,D_h,\qquad
  E_{s's}=\exp\!\Bigl[K_d\sum_k s'_k\,(s_k+s_{k+1})\Bigr],\qquad
  D_h=\operatorname{diag}\exp\!\Bigl[K_h\sum_k s_k s_{k+1}\Bigr],
  \label{eq:M1M2}
\end{equation}
with $s_{m+1}=s_1$ (ring).  The full period map is
\begin{equation}
  \Ccomp=M_1M_2=\Wz^L\,E\,D_h\,E^{\mathsf T}D_h
       =M_1^2\,\tau,
  \label{eq:periodmap}
\end{equation}
where $M_2=\Wz^mE^{\mathsf T}D_h=M_1\tau$ and $\tau$ is the bond-ring shift
($(E^{\mathsf T}=E\tau$, $[D_h,\tau]=0)$.  The bond weight of a single pair
transition factorises as $w(s'|s,s^+)$ with
\begin{equation}
  a=w(+|+,+),\qquad b=w(\pm|+,-),\qquad c=w(-|+,+),
  \label{eq:weights}
\end{equation}
and the Ising couplings are (Eq.~\ref{eq:Kdh})
\begin{equation}
  K_d=\tfrac14\ln\frac{a}{c},\qquad
  K_h=\tfrac14\ln\frac{ac}{b^2},\qquad
  \Wz=(acb^2)^{1/4}.
  \label{eq:Kdh}
\end{equation}
Writing $\vth=d-(d-1)p$, the measurement channel gives the weights in closed
form, with two branches joined continuously at the annealed critical point
$\vth_c=\beta_d+\sqrt{\beta_d^2+1}$, $\beta_d=(d^2-1)/(d^2+1)$:
\begin{align}
  \text{\emph{ordered}}\ (\vth\ge\vth_c):\qquad
  a&=\frac{(\vth+1)^2}{2(d^2+1)},& b&=\frac{\vth^2-1}{2(d^2-1)},&
  c&=\frac{(\vth-1)^2}{2(d^2+1)},&
  \Wz&=\frac{\vth^2-1}{2\sqrt{d^4-1}},
  \label{eq:wordered}\\[2pt]
  \text{\emph{disordered}}\ (\vth\le\vth_c):\qquad
  a&=\frac{d^2\vth^2-1}{d^4-1},& b&=\frac{\vth}{d^2+1},&
  c&=\frac{d^2-\vth^2}{d^4-1},&
  \Wz&=\frac{\bigl[\vth(d^2-1)\bigr]^{1/2}
        \bigl[(d^2\vth^2-1)(d^2-\vth^2)\bigr]^{1/4}}{d^4-1}.
  \label{eq:wdisordered}
\end{align}
At $\vth=\vth_c$ both branches give $a-c=2b$ exactly: the $k=0$ mode of
Prop.~\ref{prop:spectrum} is gapless there.  In the limits $p\to1$
($\vth\to1$) the three weights coincide, $a=b=c=1/(d^2+1)$ (the disordered
fixed point), and at $p\to0$ ($\vth\to d$) the ordered weights
$a=(d+1)^2/2(d^2+1)$, $b=\tfrac12$, $c=(d-1)^2/2(d^2+1)$ describe the
strong-coupling (volume-law) limit.

\begin{theorem}[rank; compressed period map]\label{thm:rank}
Let $L=2m\ge4$, $d\ge2$, $0<p<1$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $\Ccomp$ \emph{is similar to a positive-semidefinite matrix}
      $\Wz^L D_h^{1/2}YY^{\mathsf T}D_h^{1/2}$ with
      $Y=D_h^{-1/2}ED_h^{1/2}$; in particular its spectrum is real and
      non-negative.
\item[(ii)] $\Ccomp$ commutes with the global spin flip
      $F:(s\mapsto-s)$ and with the ring shift $\tau$.
\item[(iii)] The nonzero spectrum splits into a flip-even and a flip-odd
      sector of $2^{m-1}$ states each.
\item[(iv)] $\operatorname{rank}\Ccomp=3\cdot2^{m-2}$ and
      $\dim\ker\Ccomp=2^{m-2}$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(i) is the similarity $D_h^{-1/2}\Ccomp D_h^{1/2}
=\Wz^L(D_h^{-1/2}ED_h^{1/2})(D_h^{-1/2}ED_h^{1/2})^{\mathsf T}$.
(ii) follows from the invariance of the weights
$w(-s'|-s,-s^+)=w(s'|s,s^+)$ and of $D_h$ under the flip and the shift.
(iv) is proved mode-by-mode in Prop.~\ref{prop:spectrum}(iii) below (the
$k=\pi$ mode has $w_\pi^-=0$); an independent Schur-complement proof through
$L=12$ is given in App.~\ref{app:rank3}.
\end{proof}

\section{The Ising form, the Houtappel limit, and the closed-form spectrum}
\label{sec:ising}

$E$ is the diagonal-bond (vertical) transfer of an anisotropic
triangular-lattice Ising model with horizontal coupling $K_h$ and the two
diagonal couplings $K_d$; $M_1=E\,D_h$ is its row transfer.  The
finite-size scaling variable is the Ising reduced coupling; the annealed
transition is the triangular-lattice critical line
\begin{equation}
  \sinh(2K_d)\,e^{2K_h}=1,
  \label{eq:gapless}
\end{equation}
which at the physical trajectory \eqref{eq:wordered}--\eqref{eq:wdisordered}
is met exactly at $\vth=\vth_c$, i.e.\ at
$p=\pctwo(d)=(d-\vth_c)/(d-1)$: $e^{2K_h}=\beta_d$ and $\sinh(2K_d)=1/\beta_d$
there.  (For the isotropic point $K_d=K_h$ Eq.~\eqref{eq:gapless} gives
$\sinh(2K)e^{2K}=1$, i.e.\ $K=\tfrac14\ln3$, the known triangular-lattice
critical coupling.)

\begin{proposition}[Houtappel]\label{prop:houtappel}
The thermodynamic leading eigenvalue obeys
$\frac1L\ln\lambda_1\to\ln\Wz+f_H(K_d,K_h)$ with $f_H$ the anisotropic
triangular-lattice free energy per site of \citet{Houtappel1950}.  At
$d=2$, $p=0.40$: $\lambda_1(L)^{1/L}\to0.683844659$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[closed form of the nonzero spectrum]
\label{prop:spectrum}
Let $L=2m\ge4$, $d\ge2$, $0<p<1$, and let
$\Ccomp=\Wz^L E D_h E^{\mathsf T}D_h$ be the operator of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:periodmap}, with $K_d,K_h,\Wz$ as in Eq.~\eqref{eq:Kdh}.  For
$k\in[0,2\pi)$ define
\begin{equation}
  Q(k)=\cosh^2\!2K_d\,\cosh 2K_h+\sinh^2\!2K_d\,\sinh 2K_h
        -\sinh 2K_h\,\cos k,\qquad
  R(k)=2\sinh 2K_d\,\cos\frac{k}{2},
  \label{eq:QRdef}
\end{equation}
and $w_k^\pm=Q(k)\pm\sqrt{Q(k)^2-R(k)^2}$ (real, since $0\le R^2\le Q^2$ for
all $k$).  Then every nonzero eigenvalue of $\Ccomp$ is of the form
\begin{equation}
  \lambda=\bigl(2\Wz^2\bigr)^m\prod_{k\in\mathcal K_\sigma} w_k^{\pm_k},
  \label{eq:spectrum}
\end{equation}
where the product runs over the momentum states of the flip sector $\sigma$:
in the flip-even sector
$\mathcal K_+=\{(2j-1)\pi/m:\ j=1,\dots,m\}$ (antiperiodic) with an
\emph{even} number of ``$-$'' choices; in the flip-odd sector
$\mathcal K_-=\{2\pi j/m:\ j=0,\dots,m-1\}$ (periodic) with an \emph{odd}
number of ``$-$'' choices when the branches are ordered as
$w^\pm=Q\pm\sqrt{Q^2-R^2}$ fixed.  Equivalently, in the convention that
orders the $k=0$ branches by magnitude (the ordering flips at $\pctwo$),
the flip-odd sector carries an even number of ``$-$'' choices below
$\pctwo(d)$ and an odd number above, exactly as in the spinor analysis of
\citet{Kaufman1949,Newell1950}.  Moreover:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] $R(\pi)=0$, so $w_\pi^-=Q(\pi)-|Q(\pi)|=0$: every state occupying
      the ``$-$'' branch of the $k=\pi$ mode lies in the kernel.  Counting
      nonzero states (half of the sector containing $k=\pi$, all of the
      other) gives $\operatorname{rank}\Ccomp=3\cdot2^{m-2}$, recovering
      Theorem~\ref{thm:rank}(iv) by mode counting.
\item[(ii)] At $p=\pctwo(d)$ the $k=0$ mode is gapless:
      $Q(0)-R(0)$ has a double zero at $a-c=2b$, so
      $w_0^+=w_0^-$ there, and the scaled gaps have the exact limits
      $L\ln(\lambda_1/\lambda_{\rm odd})\to2\pi\,\beta_d/4$ and
      $L\ln(\lambda_1/\lambda_\varepsilon)\to2\pi\cdot2\beta_d$, ratio
      $1/8=x_\sigma/x_\varepsilon$ for every $d$.
\item[(iii)] $a-c=2b\iff Q(0)=R(0)$; the critical line
      Eq.~\eqref{eq:gapless} is the gapless condition of the $k=0$ mode.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The transfer $E$ is a bond-factorised matrix whose spinor (Jordan--Wigner)
analysis follows \citet{Kaufman1949,Newell1950,Stephenson1964,Schultz1964}:
(a)~in the spinor representation with open chain ends the diagonal transfer
is a product of Ising-Clifford exponentials.  With
$x_k=\sigma^x_k$ and $z_k=\sigma^z_k$ (the Schultz--Mattis--Lieb algebra of
the row transfer), $K_1$ the Kramers--Wannier dual of $K_d$
($\sinh2K_1\,\sinh2K_d=1$), $K_2=K_d$ and $c=\sqrt{2\sinh2K_1}$,
\begin{equation}
  E=\sinh^m(2K_d)\,\bigl[\cosh K_d\,\tilde Y+\sinh K_d\,z_m\tilde Y z_1\bigr],\qquad
  \tilde Y=c\,e^{K_1x_m}U_{m-1}\cdots U_1,\quad
  U_k=e^{K_dz_kz_{k+1}}\,c\,e^{K_1x_k},
  \label{eq:spinor}
\end{equation}
the periodic boundary contributing only through the end term $z_m\tilde Y
z_1$; every factor of \eqref{eq:spinor} has unit determinant, so
$\det\tilde Y=c^{m2^m}$, one flip sector of $E$ is exactly singular (the
$k=\pi$ kernel of item~(i) below, at the transfer level), and the two flip
sectors of the open-boundary transfer have equal determinants
$|\det E_{\rm OBC,\varepsilon}|=(2\sinh2K_d)^{m2^{m-2}}$.  Identity
\eqref{eq:spinor} is machine-verified (relative residual $\le6\times
10^{-16}$ for $m=3$--$8$, $d\in\{2,3,5\}$, both branches of the weight
trajectory; \texttt{spinor\_verify.py}, checks V1--V4).
(b)~On each flip-parity sector $\varepsilon=\pm1$, $E_\varepsilon$ commutes
with the Majorana translation $T_\varepsilon$ carrying the boundary sign
$-\varepsilon$, hence is block-diagonal in the momentum basis
$\mathcal K_\sigma$ fixed by that sign (antiperiodic for the even sector,
periodic for the odd sector).  (c)~Each momentum block of
$\Ccomp=\Wz^L(ED_h)(ED_h)^{\mathsf T}\tau$ is a $2\times2$ Majorana
quadratic form; diagonalising it gives the two eigenvalues $w_k^\pm$ of
Eqs.~\eqref{eq:QRdef}, with the half-shift $\cos(k/2)$ in $R(k)$ tracing to
$E^{\mathsf T}=E\tau$.  (d)~Multiplying the per-momentum block eigenvalues
over the sector and fixing the parity of ``$-$'' choices from the fermion
parity of the sector (the $k=0$ block changes its parity character at
$\pctwo$, the Kaufman boundary-condition switch) yields
Eq.~\eqref{eq:spectrum}.  (e)~The kernel count (i) is immediate from
$R(\pi)=0$; the amplitude limits (ii) follow from the expansion of
$\ln w_k^+-\ln w_k^-$ near $k=0$ at $\vth=\vth_c$ where
$ac/b^2=\beta_d^2$; and (iii) is a direct substitution using

Every algebraic step of the proof has been checked numerically: the spinor

$6\times10^{-16}$; the nonzero
spectrum of the dense $\Ccomp$ agrees with Eq.~\eqref{eq:spectrum} to

lies inside every exact-rational Collatz--Wielandt enclosure of

\end{proof}

\section{The annealed critical line and the effective-density mechanism}
\label{sec:annealed}

\subsection{The exact critical line}
Solving the gapless condition \eqref{eq:gapless} on the trajectory
\eqref{eq:wordered}--\eqref{eq:wdisordered} gives, for every $d\ge2$,
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\;\pctwo(d)=\frac{d-\vth_c}{d-1},\qquad
  \vth_c=\beta_d+\sqrt{\beta_d^2+1},\qquad \beta_d=\frac{d^2-1}{d^2+1}.\;}
  \label{eq:pctwo}
\end{equation}
Numerically $\pctwo(2)=0.233810$, $\pctwo(3)=0.459688$,
$\pctwo(5)=0.679004$.  For the Clifford realization of the present period
Appendix~\ref{app:numerics} finds $p_c=0.1597(8)$, against
$\pctwo(2)=0.2338$; at every size the quenched half-chain entropy lies
strictly below the annealed quasi-entropy $\tilde S_2$
(Table~\ref{tab:numerics}), whose record-collision weight favours
trajectories with fewer measurements (effective rate
$p_{\rm eff}=0.092$ at $p=0.16$; App.~\ref{app:numerics}); at
$p=0.22<\pctwo(2)$ the quenched chain is already in its area-law phase while
the annealed model is still ordered.

\subsection{Why the annealed entropy exceeds the quenched one}
\label{sec:tilt}
For stabilizer states $\tr\rho_A^2=2^{-S_A}$, so
$\bar P_{2,A}/\bar Z_2=\E_{\rm tilt}[2^{-S_A}]$ with the tilt measure
$\propto p_R^2$ (the record-collision weight), and Jensen gives
$\tilde S_2(A)=-\log_2\E_{\rm tilt}[2^{-S_A}]\le\E_{\rm tilt}[S_A]$: for a
\emph{common} measure the annealed quantity is the \emph{smaller} one.  The
observed $\tilde S_2>\E_{\rm Born}S$ therefore requires a change of measure,
and the $p_R^2$ weight supplies it: it favours records with fewer
measurements.  The exact tilted density is
\begin{equation}
  p_{\rm eff}=p+\frac{p(1-p)}{2L}\,\partial_p\ln\lambda_1(L)
  \label{eq:peff}
\end{equation}
with $\lambda_1$ the leading eigenvalue of $\Ccomp$: $0.1049$ ($L=8$),
$0.1011$ ($L=12$), $0.0920$ ($L\to\infty$) at $p=0.16$; $0.1493$ at
$p=\pctwo(2)$ (closed form and transfer matrix agree to five digits).  An
exact/sampled chain at $L=8$, $t=4$ periods, $p=0.16$ ($40{,}000$ Born
trajectories,
effective sample size $5935$) quantifies the mechanism: the exact annealed
quasi-entropy $\tilde S_2=2.155$ bits exceeds the quenched mean
$\E S=2.019(4)$ by $0.14$, while the quenched mean at the density-shifted
rate is $2.339(4)$: the density shift alone accounts for
${\approx}80\%$ of the tilt gap ($2.429-2.019=0.41$), the remainder being
record selection at fixed density (the tilted sample overshoots the exact
annealed value by $0.27$, the finite-ESS convexity cost).  At $L=12$ the
exact numbers ($\tilde S_2=3.128$, $\E S=2.394(5)$, $S(p_{\rm eff})=2.941(4)$)
show the same ordering; the sampled tilt average there (ESS $454$) is not
reliable and is not used.  (The bare $p$ at which $p_{\rm eff}=0.1595$ in the
thermodynamic limit is $0.2446$---close to, but not equal to,
$\pctwo(2)=0.2338$; this is a numerical coincidence and does not represent a
mapping between the two transitions.)  The exact side of this chain is
reproduced from first principles in App.~\ref{app:scgf} (the two-replica
algebra evolution, which also gives the saturated values
$\tilde S_2(\infty)=2.2243$ at $L=8$ and $3.2496$ at $L=12$ and the time
convention $t=L/2$ periods of the deposited chain).

\section{Multifractal record statistics}\label{sec:records}

The Born probability of a record of the monitored circuit is a product of
per-measurement dyadic probabilities, and its statistics across records are
multifractal.  We state the deposited result in the notation of the record
literature: with $\tau(q)$ the scaling exponent of the $q$-th record moment
and $\psi(x)$ the convex conjugate of the Craps--Huberman spectrum,
\begin{equation}
  \tau(q)=-\psi(1-q)
  \label{eq:taupsi}
\end{equation}
(Theorem~\texttt{record-mf}; stated once, used consistently for all $q$).
The free-fermion determinants of Sec.~\ref{sec:ising} provide the exact
annealed side of these moments; the quenched side is sampled.

\section{The record SCGF: thermodynamic limit, freezing, and the replica
interchange}\label{sec:scgf}

This section closes the four record-level theorem items: the thermodynamic
limit of the quenched scaled cumulant generating function (SCGF) of the
record information, the freezing realization of the collision tilt, the
disorder--replica interchange, and the complexity status of the Born
averages.  Throughout, $t=\tau L$ periods ($2Lt$ measurement sites), and all
numerical values are produced and verified in the present workspace
(App.~\ref{app:scgf}).

\subsection{The dyadic record probability and the SCGF}

A computational-basis measurement on a stabilizer state either has a
deterministic outcome or is uniform on a two-outcome fibre, so the Born
probability of a record is exactly dyadic,
\begin{equation}
  P(R)=2^{-X_R},\qquad
  X_R:=\#\{\text{random outcomes along }R\},
  \label{eq:dyadic}
\end{equation}
with $X_R$ an integer tracked exactly by the tableau simulator
(Gottesman--Knill; App.~\ref{app:scgf}).  Define the \emph{record SCGF} of
the Born (quenched) ensemble,
\begin{equation}
  \Xi_L(\beta;\tau,p):=\frac{1}{2Lt}\,
    \ln\E_{\rm Born}\bigl[2^{-\beta X_R}\bigr],
  \qquad \beta\ge0,
  \label{eq:scgfdef}
\end{equation}
a convex decreasing function with $\Xi_L(0)=0$.  Its two endpoints are
exactly solvable.  At $\beta=0$ the derivative is the quenched information
density, $\Xi'_L(0)=-\ln2\,\bar x_L$ with
$\bar x_L:=\E_{\rm Born}[X_R]/(2Lt)$.  At $\beta=1$,
\begin{equation}
  \E_{\rm Born}\bigl[2^{-X_R}\bigr]
    =\E_\omega\Bigl[\sum_R p_R(\omega)^2\Bigr]=Z_2(t),
  \label{eq:beta1exact}
\end{equation}
the two-replica (record-collision) partition function: the transfer matrix
of Sec.~\ref{sec:ising} computes $\Xi_L(1)$ \emph{exactly} for every
$(L,p,t)$, and this anchor is what closes the SCGF family beyond what
Born sampling can reach (Prop.~\ref{prop:freeze} below).

\begin{proposition}[record SCGF, thermodynamic limit]\label{prop:scgf}
Let $d=2$, $t=\tau L$ with $\tau$ fixed.
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(exact $\beta=1$ endpoint)} $\E_{\rm Born}[2^{-X_R}]=Z_2(t)$,
      and $Z_2(t)=A(L,\tau,p)\,\lambda_1(L)^t$ with $A$ subexponential in
      $t$ (verified: $A=2.4512$, $4.235$, $7.229$ at $L=8,12,16$, $p=0.16$,
      constant to six digits for $t\gtrsim3L$; $A=3.279$ and $6.833$ at
      $L=8,12$, $p=0.22$).  Hence
      $\Xi_L(1;\tau)\xrightarrow[t\to\infty]{}\ln\lambda_1(L)/(2L)$, and by
      Prop.~\ref{prop:houtappel}
      $\lim_L\lim_t\Xi_L(1)=\tfrac12(\ln\Wz+f_H(K_d,K_h))$, the Houtappel
      free energy per measurement site.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(convergence, both endpoints)} At $p=0.16$, $\tau=4$:
      the exact finite-$t$ anchors give
      $\Xi_L(1)=-0.07798$ ($L=8$), $-0.07936$ ($L=12$), $-0.07990$ ($L=16$)
      nats/site, and the
      closed form gives $\ln\lambda_1/(2L)=-0.07974,-0.08062,-0.08087,
      -0.08099,-0.08101$ for $L=8,12,16,24,32$: convergence to the
      Houtappel value $-0.08101$ with an $O(1/L)$ drift (at $p=0.22$:
      $-0.10870\to-0.11082$).  The quenched density converges from the
      trajectory side:
      $\bar x_L=0.1448(1),\,0.1470(1),\,0.1480(1),\,0.1489(1)$ bits/site at
      $L=8,12,16,24$ ($4\times10^4$--$1.5\times10^4$ trajectories,
      bootstrap s.e.).
\item[(iii)] \emph{(self-averaging)} The per-site variance is
      $L$-independent: $\mathrm{Var}(X_R)/(2Lt)=0.104$--$0.106$ at
      $p=0.16$ and $0.124$--$0.125$ at $p=0.22$ for $L=8$--$24$, so
      $\mathrm{Var}(\bar x)=O(1/(Lt))\to0$: the quenched information
      density obeys a large-deviation principle with $O(1)$ fluctuations
      per site---the backbone for the replica interchange below.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[freezing of the collision tilt]\label{prop:freeze}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(exact side)} The annealed quasi-entropy of
      Sec.~\ref{sec:tilt} stays strictly above the quenched half-chain
      entropy by a growing margin: at $p=0.16$, $t=\tau L$,
      $\tilde S_2-E[S_{L/2}]=0.19$ bits at $L=8$ and $0.57$ bits at $L=12$
      (both sides exact: the algebra evolution of App.~\ref{app:scgf} and
      the saturated tilted values $\tilde S_2(\infty)=2.2243$, $3.2496$).
      The tilt does not become typical as $L$ grows.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(trajectory side)} The effective sample size of the
      collision tilt ($w_R\propto p_R$, the measure of
      Sec.~\ref{sec:tilt}) collapses: $\mathrm{ESS}=12$, $3.9$, $1.4$,
      $1.0$ out of $B=4\times10^4,4\times10^4,3\times10^4,1.5\times10^4$
      trajectories at $L=8,12,16,24$ ($p=0.16$, $\tau=4$): the tilted
      measure is carried by $O(1)$--$O(10)$ records, and the naive
      trajectory estimator of $\E[P(R)]$ degrades from $0.7\%$ accuracy at
      $L=8$ to edge domination at $L\ge16$ (the trajectory $\Xi_L(1)$
      drifts below the exact anchor as its ESS collapses).  This is the
      operational signature of the frozen phase of the record ensemble, in
      the sense of the random-energy model \citep{Derrida1981}: the
      annealed observable is realised by a vanishing minority of records.
\item[(iii)] The freezing point of the multifractal spectrum
      (Sec.~\ref{sec:records}, Theorem \texttt{record-mf}) is not
      computable within the $n=2$ closure and is not claimed; what
      Prop.~\ref{prop:scgf}--\ref{prop:freeze} establish is its empirical
      realisation at the $n=2$ (collision) point.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[disorder--replica interchange]\label{prop:replica}
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{(finite $L$: no interchange needed)} For every finite $L$
      the replica derivative is exact: $\E_{\rm Born}[\ln P]
      =\partial_\beta|_0\ln\E_{\rm Born}[P^\beta]$, since a finite-support
      moment generating function is analytic at $\beta=0$.  The quenched
      free-energy density $-\ln2\,\bar x_L$ \emph{is} the replica
      derivative $\Xi'_L(0)$.
\item[(ii)] \emph{(commutation)} The thermodynamic limit commutes with the
      replica derivative on $\beta\in[0,1)$: by
      Prop.~\ref{prop:scgf}(iii) the densities $\bar x_L$ satisfy a
      large-deviation principle with vanishing relative fluctuations
      (the G\"artner--Ellis condition
      \citep{DemboZeitouni1998}), so $\Xi'_L(0)\to\Xi'(0)$ and the cumulant
      expansion
      $\Xi_L(\beta)=-\beta\ln2\,\bar x_L
      +\tfrac{\beta^2}{2}(\ln2)^2\sigma^2_{\rm eff}+O(\beta^3)$
      with $\sigma^2_{\rm eff}=\mathrm{Var}(X_R)/(2Lt)\approx0.105$
      converges.  Numerically the two-term expansion reproduces the
      measured $\Xi_L(0.25)$ to better than $1\%$ at every $L=8$--$24$
      (e.g.\ $-0.02351$ predicted vs $-0.0236(2)$ measured at $L=8$).
\item[(iii)] \emph{(the gap persists)} The interchange nonetheless fails
      to make the annealed endpoint tight: the Jensen gap
      \begin{equation}
        g_L:=\Xi_L(1)+\ln2\,\bar x_L
        =\frac{1}{2Lt}\Bigl(\ln\E[P]-\E[\ln P]\Bigr)
        \label{eq:gap}
      \end{equation}
      equals $0.0224$, $0.0226$, $0.0227$ nats/site at
      $L=8,12,16$ ($p=0.16$, $\tau=4$; exact finite-$t$ $\beta=1$ anchors at
      $L\le16$ — the $3^{16}$-configuration evolution — with trajectory
      $\bar x_L$) and $\approx0.0228$ at $L=24$ (the $A(L)$-corrected
      $\lambda_1$ anchor): $L$-independent within the trajectory errors
      ($\le5\times10^{-4}$).
      The leading cumulant $(\ln2)^2\sigma^2_{\rm eff}/2=0.0252$
      overestimates $g$ by $\approx13\%$---the record distribution is
      sub-Gaussian in the convexity-relevant tail.  (At $p=0.22$ the same
      construction gives $g\approx0.026$--$0.027$.)
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}

\begin{remark}[Born-average hardness]\label{rem:hardness}
The three levels of the complexity trichotomy of the Born averages:
(i)~\emph{Clifford realization}: $P(R)$ is dyadic, Eq.~\eqref{eq:dyadic},
and both the Born sampling and every tilted average
$\E_{\rm Born}[P^\beta f]$ are poly-time by the Gottesman--Knill theorem
\citep{Gottesman1998}---the $475{,}600$-trajectory quenched locator and the
exact transfer matrix both live in the efficiently simulable class; the
dyadic identity is verified operationally by the agreement of the tableau
estimate $\E[2^{-X_R}]$ with the transfer-matrix $Z_2$ ($0.3$--$0.7\%$ with
$4\times10^4$ trajectories at $L=8,12$; App.~\ref{app:scgf}).
(ii)~\emph{any exact $2$-design realization}: the $\beta=1$ endpoint---the
annealed collision $Z_2$---coincides with the Haar value
(Remark~\ref{rem:clifford}), so the exact solvability of the annealed
problem is not a Clifford artifact.
(iii)~\emph{Haar realization}: the per-record probabilities are the Born
probabilities of random quantum circuits; approximating them is the stock
average-case-hard task of the sampling literature
\citep{AaronsonArkhipov2013,Bouland2019} (worst-case \#P-hard; average-case
hardness conditional on standard conjectures), so no classical poly-time
method is known for the quenched Haar locator.  The Clifford testbed of
this paper is the maximal exactly-solvable window of the general monitored
problem, and the SCGF family \eqref{eq:scgfdef} is well defined precisely
because of level (i).
\end{remark}

deposited): the algebra evolution reproduces the deposited tilt chain

\section{The quenched locator}\label{sec:quenched}

The quenched ensemble is simulated by stabilizer (tableau) dynamics with
Born-sampled measurement outcomes: $L=16$--$512$, $p=0.14$--$0.18$,
$t=2L$ and $4L$, $N=800$--$4000$ trajectories per $(L,p,t)$ cell
($475{,}600$ trajectories in total), each gate an independent uniformly
random two-qubit Clifford (sampled as the $720$ symplectic actions, under
which every recorded quantity is invariant).  Trajectory $k$ of file
$(L,p)$ is generated by the legacy MT19937 stream seeded with
$\mathit{seed0}(L,p)+k$, $\mathit{seed0}$ stored in the file; regeneration
is bit-exact (checked for $23$ trajectories spanning $L=16$--$512$,
including the $I_3\equiv0$ file at $L=384$, $p=0.18$).

Table~\ref{tab:numerics} lists the crossings of $I_3(L)$ and $I_3(2L)$

the corrected, tail-safe fits centre at $0.1597$--$0.1598$ and every

removed or replaced by a spline scaling function, which leaves the answer
unchanged (report, Sec.~3.5).  Adopted:
$\nu=1.24\pm0.07$, covering the full tail-safe envelope $1.21$--$1.29$;
$\nu=1$ is excluded and the percolation value $4/3$ is disfavoured
($\Delta\chi^2=3.0$--$36.5$, never excluded by the weakest variant).

\paragraph{Logarithmic entanglement.}
The straight-line fits give $\alpha=1.58$--$1.66$ at $p_c=0.1595$ but

($y=0.5,1,2$) is never preferred and moves $\alpha$ by $<0.05$.

\paragraph{Annealing versus quenching.}
The exact annealed quasi-entropy $\tilde S_2(A)$ exceeds the quenched
$\E_\omega S(A)$ at every size and $p$; the excess is a density effect
(Sec.~\ref{sec:tilt}): the record-collision weight of the annealed channel,
proportional to $p_R^2$, favours trajectories with fewer measurements, and
the effective monitoring rate of the tilted ensemble is
$p_{\rm eff}=p+\frac{p(1-p)}{2L}\partial_p\ln\lambda_1$
($0.105$ at $L=8$, $0.101$ at $L=12$, $0.092$ in the $L\to\infty$ limit at
$p=0.16$).  A $40{,}000$-trajectory Born chain at $L=8$, $t=4$ periods (ESS
$5935$) quantifies this (Sec.~\ref{sec:tilt}); at $L=12$ the exact values
are used.  Haar-gate statevector runs with Born sampling ($L=8,12,16$) show
the same ordering of annealed and quenched entropies, but these sizes are
too small for an independent Haar transition point; the Haar values
$p_c^{\rm H}=0.17(1)$, $\nu^{\rm H}=1.2(2)$ are those of \citet{Zabalo2020}.

\paragraph{Literature and the independent purification locator.}
Gullans and Huse report $p_c=0.1593(5)$, $\nu=1.28(2)$ ($I_3$ crossing,
pure states; their purification protocol locates the same point through the
mixed-state order parameter); the coefficient $\tilde c\approx1.5$--$1.6$
quoted in that literature equals our $\alpha$ in units of $\ln 2$.
Sierant et al.\ report $0.15995(10)$.  The present values agree with all of
them within the stated uncertainties.

A locator based on a different observable, and on trajectories disjoint from
the $475{,}600$ above, is the Gullans--Huse reference-qubit protocol
\citep{Gullans2020}: the $L$-qubit system starts in the completely mixed
state, purified by $L$ reference qubits ($2L$ qubits in total), and the same
circuit and measurements act on the system only, so that $S_{\rm ref}(t)$,
the entropy of the reference, is the entropy of the system's mixed state.
We ran $L=16$--$256$, $p=0.14$--$0.18$, $2000$--$4000$ trajectories per
point (independent seeds, disjoint from the $I_3$ blocks), recording
$S_{\rm ref}$ at $t=\tau L$, $\tau=0.25,0.5,1,2,4$ ($135{,}000$
trajectories, $270$ files; \texttt{mipt\_purif\_sim.py}).  At $p=0.16$ the
reference entropy is nearly $L$-independent for $\tau\ge1$ and decays with
$\tau$ as $F(0,\tau)\sim1/\tau$.  Pair crossings of the $L\ge64$ curves give
$0.1606$--$0.1628$; the collapses $S_{\rm ref}=F((p-p_c)L^{1/\nu})$ with a
sextic $F$ and $L\ge64$ give $p_c=0.16039$, $\nu=1.251$
($\chi^2/{\rm dof}=15.5/18$) at $\tau=0.5$ and $p_c=0.16009$, $\nu=1.249$
($18.3/18$) at $\tau=1$; a trajectory bootstrap ($100$ resamples) gives
$0.16011(9)$, $1.250(14)$ ($\tau=1$, $L\ge64$).  The $\tau=2$ collapse
($0.16027$, $1.310$, $\chi^2/{\rm dof}=82.8/18$) is degraded by the same
saturated-tail pathology as in Sec.~3.5 of the report and is not used.
Frozen-$\nu$ $\Delta\chi^2$ on the $L\ge64$ set ($\tau=1$): $\nu=1$: $544$;
$1.2$: $16.9$; $1.24$: $0.5$; $1.28$: $6.6$; $4/3$: $45.8$; $1.4$: $139$.
We therefore find
\begin{equation}
  p_c^{\rm purif}=0.1601\text{--}0.1604,\qquad
  \nu^{\rm purif}=1.25(2),
  \label{eq:purif}
\end{equation}
from an observable and a trajectory set independent of the $I_3$ headline
above; it is quoted separately and does not enter the $p_c=0.1597(8)$ of the
$I_3$/entropy locators (different observable, different finite-size
corrections; combining them would hide that).

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

The annealed two-replica problem of the monitored Clifford circuit is
exactly solvable: a three-projector per-site algebra compresses to a
triangular-lattice Ising transfer whose complete nonzero spectrum has the
closed form of Prop.~\ref{prop:spectrum}, whose critical line is exact for
every local dimension, and whose leading eigenvalues are certified in exact
rational arithmetic.  The quenched transition sits at $p_c=0.1597(8)$ with
$\nu=1.24(7)$ and $\alpha=1.55(7)$ bits per $\ln L$, against the exact
annealed $\pctwo(2)=0.2338$; the record-collision weight resolves the
annealed--quenched entropy ordering through an effective-density mechanism
rather than a convexity argument; and an independent purification locator
with disjoint seeds confirms the critical point through a different
observable.  The exact finite-size amplitudes at $\pctwo$
($\beta_d/4$ and $2\beta_d$, ratio $1/8$) and the multifractal record
spectrum connect the measurement problem to the exactly known Ising
corrections.  At the record level, the SCGF of Sec.~\ref{sec:scgf} closes
the loop between the two attacks: its $\beta=1$ endpoint \emph{is} the
two-replica partition function (the exact side), its $\beta=0$ derivative
\emph{is} the quenched information density (the sampled side), the
collision tilt freezes onto $O(1)$ records, and the annealed-minus-quenched
free-energy gap per site persists at $0.0222(5)$\,nats in the thermodynamic
limit---the record measure is a glass whose observables self-average, and
the Gottesman--Knill dyadic structure is what makes the whole family well
defined and efficiently computable for the Clifford realization.

\subsection{Audit-driven robustness (summary)}
Three audit rounds flagged (i) an error floor that let a single saturated
point ($I_3\equiv0$ in all $800$ trajectories at $L=384$, $p=0.18$) carry
$98\%$ of one fixed-set fit's weight; (ii) a sextic scaling function forced
through the area-law tail, tilting the collapse core and biasing
$\nu$ upward to $1.30$--$1.35$; and (iii) a mis-attributed convexity
mechanism for the annealed--quenched ordering.  The resolutions---the $1/N$
floor, tail-safe (spline or tail-excluded) fits centring at
$p_c=0.1597$--$0.1598$, $\nu=1.21$--$1.29$, and the effective-density
mechanism of Sec.~\ref{sec:tilt}---are implemented throughout; the headline
numbers are the envelope of the tail-safe analyses, not of any single fit.

$\Delta S$ crossings, the joint entropy fit---are computed from the same
$475{,}600$ trajectories; the only independent observable is the
purification protocol of Sec.~\ref{sec:quenched}, which uses disjoint seeds
and a mixed initial state.

\section{The exact two-replica evolution and the record-SCGF verification}
\label{app:scgf}

\subsection{The algebra evolution}
The annealed quantities of Sec.~\ref{sec:scgf} are computed exactly by
evolving the two-replica state on the per-site algebra
$\{e_0,e_+,e_-\}^{\otimes L}$ (the $3^L$ product basis): the gate-bond
twirl is the exact factorised projection
$\alpha I+\beta S$ with
$\alpha=(4t_it_j-s_is_j)/60$, $\beta=(4s_is_j-t_it_j)/60$ ($d=2$;
per-site $(t,s)$: $e_0\colon(2,2)$, $e_+\colon(1,1)$, $e_-\colon(1,-1)$),
the measurement layer is diagonal
($e_0\mapsto e_0$, $e_\pm\mapsto(1-p)e_\pm$: the measure/no-measure choice
is a single event shared by both replicas), and the initial state
$|0\cdots0\rangle\langle0\cdots0|^{\otimes2}$ maps, after the (inert) first
measurement layer and the first gate layer, to
$\otimes_{\text{even bonds}}[(e_0+e_+)^{\otimes2}+e_-^{\otimes2}]/10$ (trace
$1$ exactly).  The observables are linear functionals:
$Z_2=\sum_a c_a\prod_k t_{a_k}$ and
$P_{2,A}=\sum_a c_a\prod_k t_{a_k}\prod_{k\in A}\varepsilon_{a_k}$ with
$\varepsilon=(1,1,-1)$, the last being the exact algebraic form of the
replica swap $S_A$.
