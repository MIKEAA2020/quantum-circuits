## 1. The problem, exactly

The decisive eigenvalues λ₁, λ₂ (the two-phase gap
gap₁₂ = log(λ₁/λ₂)) live in the momentum-zero colour-trivial (mom0
triv.triv) sector of the compressed one-period operator
C = M1·M2 on bond-label space (S₅)^nb, nb = L/2
(`gap_utils.py`, Eq. (Wpn)):


    M2[τ;π] = ∏_k W[τ_k, π_k, π_{k+1}],   M1[π';τ] = ∏_k W[π'_k, τ_{k-1}, τ_k],
    W[π,μ,ν] = Σ_s Wg(π⁻¹s)·T[s,μ]·T[s,ν],
    Wg = the Weingarten function of U(4) on S₅ (pinv branch),
    T[s,μ] = (1−p)·2^{c(s⁻¹μ)} + 2p.


The sector is the orbit space of G = (S₅×S₅)⋊Z_nb acting on (S₅)^nb
(componentwise left/right relabeling + ring shift). Its dimensions
(the v32 probe, exact by Burnside, validated against the deposited L=8
anchor): **K(L=8) = 3,865 · K(L=10) = 346,177** (full spaces
N = 120⁴ = 2.07e8 and 120⁵ = 2.49e10; reduction factor 71,879 at L=10).

## 2. The structural factorization (validated this session)

Write ω = Wg (a class function) and ψ = T (a class function). Then


    W[π,μ,ν] = Σ_s ω(π⁻¹s) ψ(s⁻¹μ) ψ(s⁻¹ν)
    ⇒   M2 = Ω ∘ B,      M1 = Ω ∘ B′,


where Ω[s;t] = ∏_k ω(s_k⁻¹t_k) is the **per-factor class-kernel
convolution** (hence diagonal in the Peter-Weyal basis: scalar
ω̂(λ) = (1/d_λ)Σ π ω(π)χ_λ(π) per irrep λ of S₅), and


    B[s;π] = ∏_k ψ(s_k⁻¹π_k) ψ(s_k⁻¹π_{k+1})


is the **ψ-ring transfer** (the App-A triangular structure; at n=2 it
is the row-to-row Ising transfer). B′ is the input-shifted mirror.

bijection** (slice × S₅) ↔ (S₅)^nb, π = x·ρ with ρ = (e, ρ₂..ρ_nb),
under which the B-kernel's π-dependence factorizes rank-1:
B[s; x·ρ] = ∏_j f_j(ρ_j) — the input contractions are rank-1
tensordots over the (S₅)^{nb−1} slice.

## 3. The K×N wall (why L=10 resists brute force)

The invariance of the Krylov vectors (constant on G-orbits, 71,879×
compressible in *storage*) does **not** compress the *transfer
computation*: the kernel is not orbit-diagonal, and the slice
reformulation is self-similar — the output contraction of the rank-1
input contraction is itself another B-type transfer (checked
algebraically this session; three independent cost derivations agree).
Consequently every exact route that avoids representation theory pays
one of:

- **K×N per matvec** (each full-space configuration's contribution
  must be binned against all K orbit representatives):
  346,177 × 2.49e10 × 5 = 4.3e16 MACs;
- **K² storage** for the restricted block: 2 × (346,177)² × 8 B =
  1.7 TiB;
- **N-dim Krylov vectors**: 2.49e10 × 8 B = 185 GB each.

## 4. The measured cost model (validated)

The deposited L=8 rung (`v22_n5_L8_rung.json`) took 208–487 s per
p-point; the assembly model K×N×nb×2 MACs gives 6.41e12 MACs at L=8 —
**median throughput 2.5e10 MAC/s** on this box class (the model
reproduces every deposited timing within its warmup spread).
Extrapolation to L=10 at that throughput:

| route | cost | wall-clock (this box) |
|---|---|---|
| dense restricted block | 1.7 TiB × 2 | impossible (3 GB RAM) |
| streaming assembly (per p-point) | 8.6e16 MACs | ~40 days |
| streaming matvec → Lanczos(60) | 4.3e16 MACs/matvec | ~20 days/matvec → ~3.3 years |
| full-space iterative (RingOperator) | 185 GB vectors; 7.2e14 MACs/matvec | memory-impossible; 7.9 h/matvec ignoring memory |

**Verdict for this sandbox: compute does not permit.** (The honest
boundary of "if compute permits".)

## 5. The two routes that fit adequate hardware

**Route A — distributed streaming (code exists; needs cores).** The
deposited `assemble_block` machinery with its chunk-checkpoint
protocol, parallelized over chunks (the chunk pass is embarrassingly
parallel; partial orbit-binned sums reduce at the end). At 4.3e16
MACs/matvec: ~30 min/matvec on ~10³ cores → a 60-step Lanczos per
p-point in ~1–2 days of wall-clock on a persistent ~10³-core
allocation; the 9-point locator grid in ~2 weeks. Memory per node
modest (no K×K block is ever formed — the streaming-matvec variant).
Alternatively the **full-space iterative route** (Route B), unchanged
deposited code with distributed 185 GB Krylov vectors: 7.2e14
MACs/matvec → ~5 min on 10³ cores → Lanczos(60) ≈ hours per p-point.
Both routes are engineering (parallelism), not new mathematics.

**Route B′ — the representation-theoretic colour basis (the "right"
method; new implementation).** In the Peter-Weyal/CG basis the
invariant sector decomposes as ⊕ over irrep tuples
(λ₂..λ_nb) of g(λ-tuple; triv)² blocks (S₅: 7 irrep types, dims
1–6; g ≤ ~5 — blocks of size ≤ ~25); Ω is diagonal per factor
(scalar ω̂(λ)); B is the ψ-ring transfer, whose matrix elements
between CG-coupled invariant tensors close through S₅ Clebsch-Gordan
contractions — an MPS-style sweep with small bond dimensions. The
matvec then costs O(K × poly(small)) — seconds-to-minutes on ONE box.
Implementation: S₅ CG machinery + invariant tensors + the sweep
(~600–1000 lines), validated up the ladder nb=2/3/4 against the
deposited spectra — **the first rung of that ladder (the factorization
identities the route builds on) is exactly what §2 validates.**
Estimated: a dedicated engineering round.
