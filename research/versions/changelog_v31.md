## 1. The four misapplications, removed

1. **"The primitive Born law and self-existence" (para 4).** The
   sentence reading the primitivity of the Born rule as a "resonance
   with the attribute of self-existence … flagged as an analogy" is
   **deleted**. The paragraph is retitled **"The primitive Born
   rule."** Every physics statement is carried verbatim: the no-go as
   the precise statement of non-recoverability from unconditioned
   data; the Mellin derivative's asymmetric record-resolved factor;
   the annealed ladder
   $0.2338\to0.305(3)\to0.383\to0.47$ against $0.1597(8)$; the
   tilted-variance bridge; the $m\to\infty$ overshoot to the extremal
   value.

2. **"Non-eliminable division at the level of appearance" (para 3).**
   The v30 text read `thm:no-go` as a parallel to the metaphysics
   ("the view from nowhere loses exactly the information that the
   conditioned view carries … it marks, inside physics, the same
   boundary that the metaphysics draws absolutely"). The author's
   diagnosis: **inverted** — the Absolute has no division; the no-go
   exhibits division, which is a feature of *appearance*. The
   paragraph is retitled **"Division in the appearance."**: the
   metaphysics assigns division, multiplicity, and relation to
   appearance; the theorems state how division functions there
   (no-go: symmetric unconditioned data do not determine the
   Born-weighted entropy; bivariate: record-resolved data do); the
   statement carries no claim about the Absolute, "whose undividedness
   the display neither echoes nor mirrors."

3. **"The one and the many" (para 5).** Retitled **"Self-averaging:
   the ensemble and the single stream."** The one/many framing is
   **deleted** ("many-ness of realizations is statistically
   surplus"; "the metaphysics takes it as one appearing as many, and
   self-averaging is the measurable convergence condition between the
   two descriptions"). Self-averaging is now stated as what it is:
   the exact statistical condition under which the disorder-averaged
   and single-realization descriptions of the record statistics
   coincide, with the measured $L$-independent $0.0226(2)$
   nats-per-site Clifford disorder gap as one failure instance. The
   readings' relocated places are carried: Everett branches as "a
   structure of the display's grammar, not an ultimate multiplicity";
   QBist agents as experience taken as many with self-averaging the
   measurable condition under which agents holding different
   histories converge to the same probabilities; Bohm/GRW as surplus
   phenomenal structure with the anchor identities as the benchmarks
   they must reproduce or break; einselection with its controlled
   laboratory and the transition as the phase boundary of conditional
   entropy — each "claiming only what the statistics support."

4. **"Virtuous circularity, honored exactly" (para 6).** Retitled
   **"The exactness program."** The identification of the program
   with the Absolute's virtuous circularity ("has a methodological
   echo in this paper's exactness program … That is the quantitative
   form of that circularity") is **deleted**. The commitments are
   stated in their own terms — SMC estimates the record law from
   record samples with the finite-population contract stated, not
   hidden; every exact number is regenerable from the deposited
   artifact; every certificate is an integer statement; the Born rule
   is declared primitive rather than disguised as derived — and closed
   as "commitments of practice---checkability, regenerability,
   declared primitives---not a participation in the Absolute's
   self-identity."

## 2. The deeper pattern, removed

- **Para 2** (relocation of the readings): the tail that read the
  record process as "the appearance-stream in exact mathematical
  form---succession …, resolution …, and immutable law (…, and the
  no-freezing dichotomy … as timeless characterizations of the
  temporal stream)" — mapping the Absolute's immutability and
  timelessness onto the rate laws — is replaced by the author's
  characterization of appearance: "The appearance itself, in the
  metaphysics, is the Absolute's display: not a copy of it and not a
  mirror of it---\emph{maya}: neither real nor unreal, neither
  identical with nor different from the Absolute---and its structure
  is its own: division, relation, time, change.  No feature of the
  display is required to reflect an attribute of the Absolute, and
  none is taken to." The Born record is now stated "in the display's
  own terms."
- **Para 7** (Reconciliation): "the grammar of the Absolute's
  self-appearance" and "the conditions of coincidence" are reworded
  to appearance-level terms ("the paper describes that appearance …
  in exact form and in its own terms"; "the exact conditions under
  which distinct descriptions of the record statistics coincide");
  an explicit closing non-claim is added: "nothing in the work
  asserts that the appearance mirrors, echoes, or honors the
  Absolute."
- **Intro spine:** "Sec.~\ref{sec:interpretation} states the standing
  metaphysics and its reconciliation conditions" → "states the
  standing metaphysics, locates the readings of quantum mechanics at
  the level of appearance, and draws no structural parallel between
  the display and the Absolute."
